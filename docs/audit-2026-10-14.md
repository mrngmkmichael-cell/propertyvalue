# UKPropertyInsight audit, 14 October 2026

Asked by Michael after a week away: how are SEO and everything else
doing, why there has been no income, and what would make a big
difference quickly. Every figure below was read on 14 Oct 2026 from the
production database (with /admin's own functions, test accounts
excluded), the Search Console export of 5 Oct, and checks run against
the live site.

## The short answer

The website is healthy. It is not broken, it is not slow, and its data
is current. The reason there is no income is that very few people
arrive: about 70 real page views a day. At that volume a good page
converts two people a week into accounts and almost nobody into a
payment. Two faults are also costing us, and both can be fixed: half of
the people who sign up by email never see their free full report, and
the "Nearby essentials" and "Getting around" cards read "Data
unavailable" on new reports because OpenStreetMap's main service has
blocked this site.

The biggest lever we control today is the one that has always worked:
a Xiaohongshu post. Every sign-up burst has come within two days of
one, and there has been no post since 25 September.

## Faults found

**1. Half of email sign-ups never get their free full report.** Since
7 Sep the free full report is the reward for confirming the email
address (your rule). Of the 6 people who signed up with an email
address since 2 Oct, 3 used their free report. Of the other 3, one never
confirmed, and two confirmed 9 and 44 minutes later but never came
back to the house they were looking at. People who sign up with Google
skip the step. The free report is the moment the product shows what it
is worth, and a person who never reaches it has no reason to come back.

**2. OpenStreetMap's main service blocks this site by name.** Overpass
(overpass-api.de) now refuses any request that identifies as
"UKPropertyInsight", "UKProperty" or "PropertyValue", and also the
"curl" identity the code switched to after an earlier block, while a
made-up app name gets through. That is a deliberate block by the
people who run it, so the site must not disguise itself to get round
it. The other four mirrors time out, are whitelist-only, or hold Swiss
data only. Result: on new reports "Nearby essentials" (shops, GP,
pharmacy) and "Getting around" (nearest stations) read "Data
unavailable", and which way a home faces is missing. Those are Premium cards, so paying customers
are getting less than they paid for.

**3. About 5% of school page titles are cut before the distance.** Long
names such as "Sheering CofE Voluntary Controlled Primary School" push
the title past what Google shows, and the distance, the reason to click,
is the part that disappears. Six of 124 sampled pages; about 180 of the
3,627 school pages.

Not a fault: council tax page descriptions run to 180 to 206
characters on purpose, so that every band's price comes first and
shows even when Google cuts the end.

## What would make the most difference

In order of what it is likely to do for income, with the honest
caveat that nothing on the site can create money in a day at 70 page
views. Reach comes first.

1. **Post on Xiaohongshu this week, then every 3 to 5 days.** The 5 Sep
   checklist post brought 20 sign-ups in two days; the four posts after
   it were each followed by 1 to 3. Secondary school applications in England close on
   **31 October**, and the site holds the last distance offered for
   3,627 schools, so the timely post is a checklist parents keep:
   "before you choose a secondary school, check these five numbers".
   I can build the cards and the text today.
2. **Let new accounts open their free full report straight away**, and
   keep email confirmation for alerts only. Since 2 Oct only half of
   email sign-ups have seen the full report; this would let all of them.
3. **Replace the blocked OpenStreetMap lookups with official data.**
   Nearest stations from NaPTAN, the Department for Transport's
   register of every stop and station (open data, imported once and
   refreshed monthly, so no service can block it). Nearby places from
   official registers already used elsewhere on the site (food hygiene
   for shops and restaurants, NHS for GPs and pharmacies). Which way a
   home faces has no official national source, so it says so in words
   until one exists. The cards already say "Data unavailable" rather
   than showing a blank, so nothing misleads in the meantime.
4. **Send me a Search Console export** (Performance, last 3 months, and
   Indexing > Pages), so the weekly loop can promote the districts and
   schools that are earning, as it did in September.
5. **Fix the long school titles** so the distance always shows. No
   decision needed; done in this session.
6. **Outreach to firms:** 0 replies from 30. Either pause the daily
   drafts or send one short follow-up to the 30 and then decide.

## Not recommended

- A redesign. The pages people reach are read and work on a phone;
  the constraint is how many people arrive.
- Re-running email campaigns to existing accounts. The site promises
  alerts are never sent on a schedule, and nobody opted in to
  marketing.
