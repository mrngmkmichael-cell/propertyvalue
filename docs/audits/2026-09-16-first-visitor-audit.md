# First-time visitor audit, 16 September 2026

How this was done: one scripted walk through the live site as a first-time visitor, on a 1280px desktop and a 375px phone, read-only, with the internal header so it counted as no traffic. Pages: the homepage, the KT3 4HX report (cold, then warm), Premium, sign-up, a school page reached the way Google sends people (Pebble Brook Primary School), the schools guide for KT3, the KT3 area guide, running costs for KT3 4HX, alternatives, methodology, the buying guide and the admissions index. What a signed-in reader sees was read from the templates. Every figure below was measured or read on the page on 16 September 2026; screenshots are in the session's scratchpad folder `visit/out`.

The owner's own numbers, which the recommendations lean on: nobody has ever subscribed on a first visit, and every paying account came back on another day; on 14 September, 12 sign-up page views produced 2 accounts; in the last 14 days 25 accounts opened Premium and 23 have not paid; 3 of the last 4 new accounts opened Premium within seconds or minutes of using their free full report, and none bought.

## What a first-time visitor feels

I arrive on the homepage and understand it in one look: "Prices, schools, and the running costs no listing shows you", then "Forty-four checks on any UK address, each from the body that published it. Whether the house gets a child into the school. What it costs to live there. Free, and no account needed." The postcode box is above the fold on both screens. On the phone the page is ready in 1.4 seconds. Under the box, three pillars tell me what the site holds (44 checks and 2,943 area guides; 3,627 admission distances; council tax for 350 councils). I trust it faster than I expected to: the source is named in every sentence. The one line that makes me squint is the offer: "Sign up free and get a full Premium property report on us, normally £9.99/month". A report is not a monthly thing.

I type KT3 4HX. A wait page with a dial ticks off 19 sources; a cold report took 8.6 seconds, and the same report opened in 2.6 seconds the second time. The score reads well: "88/100 EXCELLENT: 90% of nearby schools rated Outstanding or Good; Lower crime than the surrounding area; Area prices rising (Kingston upon Thames, +2.9% YoY)". Under it, the bills: "£2,609 a year council tax at Band D, £1,867 a year energy, the EPC's estimate, Freehold at the last recorded sale (2025)". Then a black rectangle sits where I expect a map. It stays black for 3.6 seconds while a globe spins and fades, on every report I open, before the map appears. I do not know what it is for.

Before I reach a single card, I have been asked four things: "Coming back to this one? Save it free" on the score, "Log in to keep this property in My properties" under the map, a locked "PDF report · Premium" chip at the top right (on the phone it is the first button after the breadcrumb), and a banner, "The locked cards below are free to open. Sign up and your first property gets every check in full, this one included. No card needed. Unlock this report free". Then the six group tiles, which I like: "Risk & Safety, 11 cards, 5 locked" tells me what is inside before I open it, and a flagged group would wear a red ring. Eighteen of the 45 cards are locked, and each one carries the same pill, "Sign up: 1 free full report". The offer is generous, but repeated eighteen times under four other asks it reads as a wall rather than a gift.

The free cards are good and plain where they are plain: "Zone 1 (low probability), No active warnings", "Very low risk", "£823,500 last sale". Some I cannot read: "Decile 8 of 10" (which end is good?), "Report card" as an Ofsted grade, "72 dB(A) at its loudest", "Born outside the UK up 8.1 points". The Flood risk popup is 186 words, names the Environment Agency, explains Flood Re, ends with "Is something on this card wrong? Tell us", and closes on Escape. That is the moment I decide the site is honest. "Questions to ask, from this report's findings" promises four questions and shows me "Built before 1970", which is a heading, not a question. "Keep exploring" gives me three real next steps with real figures.

Premium says "One wrong house costs more than a lifetime of reports" and prices Premium against £25 flood reports and £50 to £110 environmental searches; the comparison table is candid ("Not listed means their pages did not describe it that day, not that they lack it"). Sign-up says "One full report, free. No card, nothing to cancel, and no clock running down while you are not house-hunting", which is the best sentence on the site for someone like me, but the page does not say which property my free report will be, even though it knows (the link carries the postcode).

Landing on a school page from Google is the strongest arrival of all: "0.02 mi, admitted from, 2025/26, about 34 metres", a postcode check, the application deadline, and "This is not a catchment area. Most English schools do not have one." I would share that page. What it does not do is tell me the site will also check the house.

What I liked, briefly: the sources on every line; "Nothing is modelled"; the running-costs opening ("£3,583 a year, 3.9% of the typical household income here"); the area guide's first paragraph; the alternatives table; and the sign-up promise. Nothing on the site tried to sell me a house.

## What would make me pay

I would sign up, without hesitation, on the report I actually care about: the offer is right and the moment is right. What slows me is the wall, not the price. I would pay £9.99 when one of three things happens: I find a second house and want them side by side; something changes on the house I saved and the site tells me; or I need the PDF for my solicitor. None of those happens on the first visit, which matches the owner's numbers exactly. So the job of the first visit is to make the second visit likely, and the job of the second visit is to put the compare, the alert and the PDF within reach.

The account data says where it goes wrong today: three of the last four new accounts went to Premium within seconds or minutes of using their free report and none bought. They were pushed to a price list at the moment they had just been given something, which is the wrong moment. A price list is for a visitor who has a second house, not one who has just finished the first.

The changes most likely to produce a second visit and then a payment:

1. One offer on the free report, not four asks and eighteen pills. Keep a lock icon on each locked card and one explained offer above the tiles. Effort S.
2. The sign-up page names the property: "Your free full report will be KT3 4HX" from the postcode already in the link. Effort S.
3. Each locked card says in one line what it would tell you, without the finding: "Which way the garden faces, from the EPC footprint", "Storm overflows within 1.5 miles, from Environment Agency returns". Eighteen lines of copy, none of them a leak. Effort M.
4. After the free unlock, the next step offered is a second property and the comparison, not Premium. Pair with the owner's item to record which link sends new accounts to Premium, so the change can be measured. Effort M.
5. Premium for an account that has used its free report should speak to that account: name the property, link back to it, say what the second one costs. Needs the owner's decision on copy. Effort M.
6. The homepage offer line: "your first property's full report free; Premium is £9.99 a month for every property". Needs the owner's decision. Effort S.
7. The free report prints properly (see High 3): the PDF a buyer takes to a viewing is the thing they show a partner, and today it comes out blank. Effort S.

## What needs improving, in priority order

### High

**Four asks before the first card, and the same lock pill eighteen times.** Observation: on the KT3 4HX report a signed-out visitor meets "Save it free", "Log in to keep this property in My properties", the locked "PDF report · Premium" chip and the banner "Unlock this report free" before the tiles, then "Sign up: 1 free full report" on 18 of 45 cards. On the phone the Premium chip is the first button after the breadcrumb. Why it matters: on 14 September, 12 sign-up page views produced 2 accounts; a gift repeated eighteen times reads as a wall. Suggestion: one explained offer above the tiles, a lock icon on each locked card, and the two save lines merged into the one on the score card. Effort S.

**The map arrives 3.6 seconds late, behind a black box, on every report.** Observation: the `globe-intro` container sits where the map goes; the script fades it out on a 3,600 ms timer with no condition, so every report open shows a black rectangle for 3.6 seconds before the map. Why it matters: the map is the second thing a buyer looks at, and a first-timer does not know the black box is decorative. Suggestion: run the globe only on the first report of a session (sessionStorage), or show the map first and let the globe play over it, or cut the timer to about 1.2 seconds. Effort S.

**Printing or saving the free report from the top gives blank sections.** Observation: with print media emulated and no scrolling, 7 of the 9 reveal-on-scroll blocks (the attention banner, highlights, paywall banner, cards, buyer questions) and all 6 group tiles are at opacity 0; the resulting A4 PDF is 772 KB of mostly empty pages. The site invites this: "Going to see it? Print a viewing checklist built from this report's findings" is on the page. Why it matters: the printout is what a buyer takes to a viewing or hands to a partner. Suggestion: a print rule that sets `[data-animate]`, `.cat-board .cat-tile` and `.cat-panel .dashboard-card` to full opacity and no animation. Effort S.

### Medium

**Card status lines that a first-timer cannot read.** Observation: "Decile 8 of 10" (no direction given), "Report card" as an Ofsted grade, "72 dB(A) at its loudest", "Born outside the UK up 8.1 points", "50.1% managerial/professional". The popups explain each, but the card is what people scan. Why it matters: the card is the promise; if it is opaque the reader does not open it. Suggestion: a plain tail on each such card ("Decile 8 of 10, where 10 is least deprived"; "Report card: inspected since Ofsted stopped one-word grades"), taken from the modal's own wording. Effort S to M.

**The buyer-questions teaser shows a heading, not a question.** Observation: "4 questions were generated for this property, including: Built before 1970. Unlock the full question list." Why it matters: the teaser is the one preview of a Premium feature on the free report, and it reads as a fragment. Suggestion: show one real question in full, for example the one generated from the build date. Effort S.

**The sign-up page does not name the property it will unlock.** Observation: the page says "Every Premium check unlocked on the first property you look at" while the link it was reached by carries `next=/property?postcode=KT3 4HX`. Why it matters: three of four recent accounts came from a locked card; naming the house closes the loop. Suggestion: "Your free full report will be KT3 4HX, this one" when `next` carries a postcode. Effort S.

**Small type on phone cards.** Observation: card titles render at 11px and lock labels at 12px on a 375px screen (measured), against 16px body text. Why it matters: the title is what a thumb scans across 45 cards. Suggestion: 12 to 13px for the title on phones, keeping the letter-spacing. Effort S.

**Thirty-eight of 84 schools on the KT3 guide show "No current grade" with no reason on the page.** Observation: the legend lists "Report card" and "No current grade" without a line saying why; the report's modal does explain that Ofsted stopped one-word grades in late 2024. Why it matters: a parent reads "no grade" as "bad" or "missing". Suggestion: one line under the legend, reused from the modal. Effort S.

**A school page does not offer the house report.** Observation: the landing screen offers the postcode check, sharing and the nearby schools, but no route to the 44 checks for the address being checked. Why it matters: school pages are where Google sends people (the site's page-1 rankings are catchment queries), and the report is where sign-ups happen. Suggestion: after a postcode is checked, one line: "Checking a house at this postcode? See its full report." Effort S.

**Long phone pages with the controls at the bottom.** Observation: the report is 7.6 screens before a group is opened and 9.9 with Risk & Safety open (11 cards, 2.3 screens); Premium is 16.3 screens, the area guide 13.5, the schools guide 16.9. The "Next" and "Back to all groups" controls sit at the foot of each group. Why it matters: on a train, a reader loses the tile row and the offer while inside a group. Suggestion: a slim bar that stays at the top while a group is open, with the group name, Close and Next. Was offered on 15 September and set aside; needs the owner's decision. Effort M.

**The homepage's decorative report card is read before the headline by a screen reader.** Observation: on the phone the extracted text begins "ukpropertyinsight.co.uk/property?postcode=M1 1AE 76 M1 1AE GOOD ..." before "Prices, schools, and the running costs". Why it matters: a screen-reader user hears a mock report before the page says what it is. Suggestion: `aria-hidden="true"` on the decorative card, or move it after the heading in the markup. Effort S.

### Low

**Two of the site's own phone controls are under 32px.** "Show every card at once" is 27px tall and the back link 21px; the other small targets are ordinary text links. Effort S.

**Unnamed map controls.** 84 `div[role=button]` elements without a name on the schools guide and 11 on the report are the map library's own marker markup, not the site's controls. Low; note only.

**Cold first load after a deploy.** The first homepage load of the walk took 3.7 seconds to DOM-ready against 0.2 to 1.5 seconds for every later page; the process had just restarted. The health check already keeps traffic on the old instance during a deploy. Note only.

## How the site could be more interactive

1. **The score's reasons open their cards.** The verdict already names its three drivers ("90% of nearby schools rated Outstanding or Good; Lower crime; Area prices rising"). Make each phrase a button to its card's popup, the same `data-modal-target` the highlights use. Real data: the report's own findings. Restraint: no motion beyond the popup that already exists. Effort S.
2. **Pick a band on the running-costs page.** Every band's bill for the council is already on the page in the row beneath the tables. A band selector (A to H) that swaps the headline "£3,583 a year" figure would let a buyer see their own bill rather than Band D. Real data: MHCLG band tables. Effort S to M.
3. **The school page hands over to the report.** After a postcode check, one tap to the 44 checks for that address, carrying the postcode. Real data: the report. Effort S.
4. **Compare two districts on the area guide.** The schools guide already has "Add area to compare"; the area guide does not. A second district beside KT3 with the same seven headline figures would answer "which area", the question the area pages get asked. Real data: the area guides. Effort M.
5. **Filter the sold-price map by year.** The comparables page holds every sale since 1995; a year range on the table and map (last 2, 5, 10 years) turns a list into a tool. Real data: HM Land Registry. Effort M.
6. **Ticks that mean something at the end.** The six-group checklist and the save line now exist; on a second visit the report could open on the group that changed, if anything did, using the saved snapshot the alerts already compare. Real data: the watchlist snapshot. Effort M, signed-in only.

What not to add: numbers that count up, confetti, sounds, auto-sliding carousels, a chatbot, or any "estimated value" dial. The first four read as generated and the design brief rules them out; the last two would break "real data or no feature".

# 初次訪客審核，2026 年 9 月 16 日

做法：以初次訪客身份按腳本走一遍正式網站，分別用 1280px 桌面和 375px 手機，唯讀，並帶內部標頭，所以不會算作流量。走訪的頁面：首頁、KT3 4HX 報告（先冷後熱）、Premium、註冊、一個從 Google 進來的學校頁面（Pebble Brook Primary School）、KT3 的學校指南、KT3 地區指南、KT3 4HX 的居住成本、alternatives、methodology、買樓指南和錄取索引。已登入用戶看到的內容是從模板讀取的。以下每個數字都是 2026 年 9 月 16 日在頁面上量度或讀到的；截圖在工作階段的 scratchpad 資料夾 `visit/out`。

## 初次訪客的感受

我到達首頁，一眼就看懂了：「Prices, schools, and the running costs no listing shows you」，然後是「Forty-four checks on any UK address, each from the body that published it. Whether the house gets a child into the school. What it costs to live there. Free, and no account needed.」郵遞區號輸入框在兩種螢幕上都不用捲動就看到。手機上頁面 1.4 秒就緒。輸入框下方的三個支柱說明網站有什麼（44 項檢查和 2,943 份地區指南；3,627 個錄取距離；350 個議會的議會稅）。我比預期更快就信任它：每句話都註明了來源。唯一讓我皺眉的是那句優惠：「Sign up free and get a full Premium property report on us, normally £9.99/month」。一份報告不是按月計的東西。

我輸入 KT3 4HX。等待頁面上的轉盤逐一勾掉 19 個來源；冷報告花了 8.6 秒，第二次打開同一份報告只需 2.6 秒。評分讀起來清楚：「88/100 EXCELLENT: 90% of nearby schools rated Outstanding or Good; Lower crime than the surrounding area; Area prices rising (Kingston upon Thames, +2.9% YoY)」。下面是開支：「£2,609 a year council tax at Band D, £1,867 a year energy, the EPC's estimate, Freehold at the last recorded sale (2025)」。然後，在我預期看到地圖的位置，是一個黑色長方形。它會維持黑色 3.6 秒，期間一個地球儀旋轉後淡出，每次打開報告都是這樣，之後地圖才出現。我不知道那是用來做什麼的。

還沒看到任何一張卡片，我已被要求了四件事：評分卡上的「Coming back to this one? Save it free」、地圖下方的「Log in to keep this property in My properties」、右上角鎖住的「PDF report · Premium」按鈕（手機上它是麵包屑之後的第一個按鈕），以及一條橫幅「The locked cards below are free to open. Sign up and your first property gets every check in full, this one included. No card needed. Unlock this report free」。接著是六個分類方塊，我很喜歡：「Risk & Safety, 11 cards, 5 locked」在打開前就告訴我裡面有什麼，有問題的分類會帶紅框。45 張卡片中有 18 張鎖住，每張都帶著同樣的標籤「Sign up: 1 free full report」。優惠很慷慨，但在四個要求之下重複十八次，讀起來像一堵牆而不是一份禮物。

免費卡片在寫得直白的地方都很好：「Zone 1 (low probability), No active warnings」、「Very low risk」、「£823,500 last sale」。有些我看不懂：「Decile 8 of 10」（哪一端是好的？）、把「Report card」當成 Ofsted 評級、「72 dB(A) at its loudest」、「Born outside the UK up 8.1 points」。Flood risk 彈窗有 186 字，註明 Environment Agency，解釋了 Flood Re，結尾是「Is something on this card wrong? Tell us」，按 Escape 可以關閉。就是在那一刻我判定這個網站是誠實的。「Questions to ask, from this report's findings」承諾四個問題，卻只給我看「Built before 1970」，那是個標題，不是問題。「Keep exploring」給了我三個有真實數字的下一步。

Premium 頁說「One wrong house costs more than a lifetime of reports」，把 Premium 和 £25 的洪水報告、£50 至 £110 的環境調查作比較；比較表很坦白（「Not listed means their pages did not describe it that day, not that they lack it」）。註冊頁說「One full report, free. No card, nothing to cancel, and no clock running down while you are not house-hunting」，對我這樣的人來說是全站最好的一句話，但頁面沒有說我的免費報告會是哪個物業，儘管它知道（連結帶著郵遞區號）。

從 Google 進入學校頁面是最有力的到達方式：「0.02 mi, admitted from, 2025/26, about 34 metres」、郵遞區號檢查、申請截止日期，以及「This is not a catchment area. Most English schools do not have one.」我會分享那個頁面。它沒有做到的是告訴我這個網站也會檢查房子本身。

簡短說說我喜歡的：每一行都有來源；「Nothing is modelled」；居住成本的開頭（「£3,583 a year, 3.9% of the typical household income here」）；地區指南的第一段；alternatives 比較表；以及註冊頁的承諾。網站上沒有任何東西試圖向我推銷房子。

## 什麼會讓我付費

在我真正在意的那份報告上，我會毫不猶豫地註冊：優惠是對的，時機也是對的。讓我放慢的是那堵牆，不是價格。以下三件事之一發生時，我會付 £9.99：我找到第二間房子，想把兩間並排比較；我儲存的房子有變化而網站通知了我；或者我需要 PDF 給律師。這些都不會在第一次到訪時發生，這和網站的數字完全吻合。所以第一次到訪的任務是讓第二次到訪更有可能發生，第二次到訪的任務是把比較、提醒和 PDF 放在觸手可及的地方。

帳戶數據說明了今天的問題出在哪裡：最近 4 個新帳戶中有 3 個在用完免費報告後幾秒或幾分鐘內就去了 Premium，沒有一個購買。他們在剛剛得到東西的那一刻被推到價目表，那是錯誤的時機。價目表是給有第二間房子的訪客看的，不是給剛看完第一間的人。

最有可能帶來第二次到訪、進而付費的改動：

1. 免費報告上只有一個優惠，而不是四個要求加十八個標籤。每張鎖住的卡片保留鎖頭圖示，方塊上方保留一段有說明的優惠。工作量 S。
2. 註冊頁註明物業：「Your free full report will be KT3 4HX」，用連結中已有的郵遞區號。工作量 S。
3. 每張鎖住的卡片用一行說明它會告訴你什麼，但不透露結果：「Which way the garden faces, from the EPC footprint」、「Storm overflows within 1.5 miles, from Environment Agency returns」。十八行文案，沒有一行洩露結果。工作量 M。
4. 免費解鎖後，提供的下一步是第二個物業和比較功能，而不是 Premium。配合網站擁有人清單中「記錄哪個連結把新帳戶送到 Premium」那一項，讓改動可以量度。工作量 M。
5. 已用完免費報告的帳戶看到的 Premium 頁應該對著這個帳戶說話：註明物業、連結回去、說明第二個物業要多少錢。文案需要擁有人決定。工作量 M。
6. 首頁優惠文字：「your first property's full report free; Premium is £9.99 a month for every property」。需要擁有人決定。工作量 S。
7. 免費報告能正常列印（見高優先第 3 項）：買家帶去看樓的 PDF 就是他們給伴侶看的東西，今天列印出來是空白的。工作量 S。

## 需要改進的地方，按優先次序

### 高

**第一張卡片之前有四個要求，同一個鎖定標籤重複十八次。** 觀察：在 KT3 4HX 報告上，未登入的訪客在方塊之前會遇到「Save it free」、「Log in to keep this property in My properties」、鎖住的「PDF report · Premium」按鈕和橫幅「Unlock this report free」，然後是 45 張卡片中 18 張上的「Sign up: 1 free full report」。手機上 Premium 按鈕是麵包屑之後的第一個按鈕。為什麼重要：9 月 14 日 12 次註冊頁瀏覽只產生 2 個帳戶；一份禮物重複十八次讀起來就像一堵牆。建議：方塊上方保留一段有說明的優惠，每張鎖住的卡片只留鎖頭圖示，兩句儲存提示合併成評分卡上的一句。工作量 S。

**地圖晚了 3.6 秒才出現，前面是一個黑色方塊，每份報告都如此。** 觀察：`globe-intro` 容器佔據地圖的位置；腳本用 3,600 毫秒的計時器把它淡出，沒有任何條件，所以每次打開報告都會先看到 3.6 秒的黑色長方形，地圖才出現。為什麼重要：地圖是買家第二個看的東西，初次訪客不知道那個黑色方塊只是裝飾。建議：地球儀只在每個工作階段的第一份報告播放（sessionStorage），或先顯示地圖讓地球儀在上面播放，或把計時器縮短到約 1.2 秒。工作量 S。

**從頂部列印或儲存免費報告會得到空白區段。** 觀察：模擬列印媒體且不捲動時，9 個捲動才顯示的區塊中有 7 個（注意橫幅、亮點、付費橫幅、卡片、買家問題）和全部 6 個分類方塊的不透明度都是 0；產生的 A4 PDF 有 772 KB，大部分是空頁。網站本身鼓勵這樣做：頁面上有「Going to see it? Print a viewing checklist built from this report's findings」。為什麼重要：列印本就是買家帶去看樓或交給伴侶的東西。建議：加一條列印規則，把 `[data-animate]`、`.cat-board .cat-tile` 和 `.cat-panel .dashboard-card` 設為完全不透明且無動畫。工作量 S。

### 中

**初次訪客看不懂的卡片狀態文字。** 觀察：「Decile 8 of 10」（沒有說明方向）、把「Report card」當作 Ofsted 評級、「72 dB(A) at its loudest」、「Born outside the UK up 8.1 points」、「50.1% managerial/professional」。彈窗都有解釋，但人們掃視的是卡片。為什麼重要：卡片就是承諾；看不懂就不會打開。建議：在這類卡片上加一句直白的補充（「Decile 8 of 10, where 10 is least deprived」；「Report card: inspected since Ofsted stopped one-word grades」），取自彈窗本身的文字。工作量 S 至 M。

**買家問題的預覽顯示的是標題，不是問題。** 觀察：「4 questions were generated for this property, including: Built before 1970. Unlock the full question list.」為什麼重要：這是免費報告上唯一一個 Premium 功能的預覽，讀起來卻像片段。建議：完整顯示一條真正的問題，例如由建造年份產生的那條。工作量 S。

**註冊頁沒有註明將會解鎖的物業。** 觀察：頁面說「Every Premium check unlocked on the first property you look at」，而它是經由帶有 `next=/property?postcode=KT3 4HX` 的連結到達的。為什麼重要：最近四個帳戶中有三個來自鎖住的卡片；點出那間房子就把迴路閉合了。建議：當 `next` 帶有郵遞區號時顯示「Your free full report will be KT3 4HX, this one」。工作量 S。

**手機卡片上的字太小。** 觀察：在 375px 螢幕上，卡片標題實際為 11px，鎖定標籤 12px（實測），而正文是 16px。為什麼重要：標題是拇指在 45 張卡片間掃視的依據。建議：手機上標題用 12 至 13px，保留字距。工作量 S。

**KT3 學校指南上 84 間學校中有 38 間顯示「No current grade」，頁面上沒有原因。** 觀察：圖例列出「Report card」和「No current grade」，卻沒有一行說明為什麼；報告的彈窗有解釋 Ofsted 在 2024 年底停止了單字評級。為什麼重要：家長會把「no grade」讀成「差」或「缺失」。建議：圖例下加一行，沿用彈窗的文字。工作量 S。

**學校頁面沒有提供房屋報告。** 觀察：到達畫面提供郵遞區號檢查、分享和附近學校，但沒有通往被檢查地址那 44 項檢查的路徑。為什麼重要：學校頁面是 Google 把人送來的地方（網站第一頁的排名都是錄取範圍查詢），而報告是註冊發生的地方。建議：檢查完郵遞區號後加一行：「Checking a house at this postcode? See its full report.」工作量 S。

**手機頁面很長，控制項在底部。** 觀察：報告在打開分類前有 7.6 屏，打開 Risk & Safety 後 9.9 屏（11 張卡片，2.3 屏）；Premium 16.3 屏，地區指南 13.5 屏，學校指南 16.9 屏。「Next」和「Back to all groups」在每個分類的底部。為什麼重要：在火車上，讀者進入分類後就看不到方塊列和優惠。建議：分類打開時頂部固定一條細列，顯示分類名稱、Close 和 Next。9 月 15 日曾提出並暫時擱置；需要擁有人決定。工作量 M。

**首頁的裝飾性報告卡在螢幕閱讀器中會先於標題被讀出。** 觀察：手機上擷取的文字以「ukpropertyinsight.co.uk/property?postcode=M1 1AE 76 M1 1AE GOOD ...」開頭，之後才是「Prices, schools, and the running costs」。為什麼重要：使用螢幕閱讀器的人會先聽到一份模擬報告，頁面才說明自己是什麼。建議：在裝飾卡上加 `aria-hidden="true"`，或在標記中把它移到標題之後。工作量 S。

### 低

**網站自己的兩個手機控制項小於 32px。** 「Show every card at once」高 27px，返回連結高 21px；其他小目標都是一般文字連結。工作量 S。

**沒有名稱的地圖控制項。** 學校指南上 84 個、報告上 11 個沒有名稱的 `div[role=button]` 是地圖程式庫自己的標記元素，不是網站的控制項。低；僅記錄。

**部署後的冷啟動首次載入。** 走訪中首頁的第一次載入用了 3.7 秒才 DOM 就緒，之後的每個頁面只需 0.2 至 1.5 秒；當時程序剛重啟。健康檢查已經能在部署期間讓流量留在舊實例上。僅記錄。

## 網站可以如何更具互動性

1. **評分的理由可以打開對應的卡片。** 結論已經列出三個驅動因素（「90% of nearby schools rated Outstanding or Good; Lower crime; Area prices rising」）。把每句話做成按鈕，打開對應卡片的彈窗，用亮點已在用的同一個 `data-modal-target`。真實數據：報告本身的結果。克制：除了已有的彈窗之外沒有額外動畫。工作量 S。
2. **在居住成本頁選擇稅級。** 該議會每個稅級的帳單已經在表格下方那一列。一個稅級選擇器（A 至 H）切換標題數字「£3,583 a year」，讓買家看到自己的帳單而不是 Band D。真實數據：MHCLG 稅級表。工作量 S 至 M。
3. **學校頁面交棒給報告。** 檢查郵遞區號後，一下就能到該地址的 44 項檢查，帶著郵遞區號。真實數據：報告。工作量 S。
4. **在地區指南比較兩個分區。** 學校指南已有「Add area to compare」，地區指南沒有。在 KT3 旁邊放第二個分區，用同樣七個標題數字，就能回答「哪個地區」，這正是地區頁面被問的問題。真實數據：地區指南。工作量 M。
5. **按年份篩選成交價地圖。** comparables 頁面持有 1995 年以來的每筆成交；在表格和地圖上加年份範圍（最近 2、5、10 年），清單就變成工具。真實數據：HM Land Registry。工作量 M。
6. **最後有意義的勾號。** 六個分類的清單和儲存提示已經存在；第二次到訪時，如果有變化，報告可以直接打開變化了的分類，用提醒功能已在比較的儲存快照。真實數據：watchlist 快照。工作量 M，僅限已登入。

不該加的：數字滾動、彩紙、音效、自動輪播、聊天機器人，或任何「估價」儀表。前四項看起來像自動生成，設計規範也不允許；後兩項會違反「真實數據或不做功能」。

## 本週的速效改動

1. 免費報告上只有一個優惠；用鎖頭圖示取代十八個標籤。
2. 一條列印規則，讓免費報告完整列印。
3. 地球儀每個工作階段只播一次，或 1.2 秒，或在地圖後面。
4. 結論的三個理由可以打開對應卡片。
5. 註冊頁註明物業。
6. 買家問題預覽顯示一條真正的問題。
7. 學校指南圖例下加一行說明「Report card」和「No current grade」。
8. 從學校頁面的郵遞區號檢查通往房屋報告。
9. 手機卡片標題 12 至 13px。
10. 首頁裝飾性報告卡加 `aria-hidden`。

## 需要你決定

- 首頁優惠文字（一份報告不是「£9.99/month」）。
- 已用完免費報告的帳戶看到的 Premium 文案。
- 手機上的固定分類列。
- 鎖住卡片上十八行「這張卡片會告訴你什麼」的說明。
