# Each page makes a promise the next page does not keep

_UKPropertyInsight · first-time visitor audit · 17 September 2026_

A first-time visitor's walk through the live site: what works, where trust breaks at each handover, what would bring me back and make me pay, and the tools that would make me stay.

How this was done: one scripted walk through the live site on 17 September 2026, as a first-time visitor with no account, on a 1280px desktop and a 375px phone. It was read-only and sent the internal header, so none of it counts as traffic. It covered 58 page steps: the homepage, a cold search and its wait, the KT3 4HX report with every group, card popup and a locked card opened, running costs, comparables, Premium, sign-up and log-in (viewed, never submitted), and the pages Google sends people to: a school catchment page, a council tax page, a council's private schools, an area guide and its comparison, an admissions hub, the schools guide, the market report, the area index and the buying guide.

What happens after signing up cannot be walked on the live site without creating an account, so that journey was rendered through the test client with made-up figures: the report before and straight after the free unlock, a second property, My properties, the comparison, Premium as a free account, and a subscriber's report. Those screens are judged on their words and offers, not their numbers.

Six reviewers read the evidence, one lens each: arrival, the report, pages from Google, paying, interactive tools and craft. A sceptical second reviewer checked every finding against the evidence, the templates and, where needed, the live site. Of 70 findings, 44 stood as written, 26 were corrected in detail and none was rejected. I then re-checked the four most serious against the live site and the templates myself, and all four stand. Duplicates across lenses are merged below.

**The numbers this leans on**

- **Never** has anyone subscribed on a first visit
- **41 of 56** real accounts (12 Sep) used the site on one day only, and none of them paid
- **2 of 15** accounts that came back on another day subscribed, both with several homes on the go
- **3** real sign-ups from 14 to 16 Sep, with 3 free unlocks
- **2** paywall views in four days, and no Premium since 6 Sep
- **10.4 s** for a cold report on a phone, from search to finished report, measured today
- **18 of 45** report cards locked for a visitor without an account

## What a first-time visitor feels

On my phone I know what this is within five seconds: "Prices, schools, and the running costs no listing shows you", one plain sentence about checks from the bodies that publish them, and a postcode box. The source is named in nearly every sentence, and a public log admits a mistake. That honesty is the best thing on the site. On the laptop, a demo report card sits above the headline and pushes the box to the bottom edge, and clicking it does nothing.

The wait for a cold report is good: a list of real sources ticks off under "Usually 5 to 15 seconds", and mine was ready in 10.4. The report opens on "88/100 EXCELLENT" and "No major red flags found". Then the doubts start. The semi-detached house, its £1,867 energy bill and its £823,500 sale come from different neighbours, because I searched a postcode without a house number. Air quality and contamination are padlocked, yet the green banner vouches for them. The locked tiles are blank, and the rent, income and stamp duty they hide are free on the next page. The questions to ask the seller sit at the very bottom, and three of the four would fit any house.

Arriving from Google is where the site shines and where it lets me down. The Fortismere page answers my catchment search on the first screen: 1.88 miles, 105% full, a postcode box. The Basildon page gives Band D to the penny. But the council tax page promises the free report shows "the band an address actually carries", and the report tells me to ask the seller. The school page tells me "Likely", and the report's schools popup says the site "can't say". On my phone the admissions table shows distances of "0" until I find that it scrolls sideways.

When I sign up, the report asks "Use your free full report on M14 5TG?" and, a scroll lower, says "You've used your free report" beside a price. After I unlock, a "Download full PDF report" button appears, as sign-up promised, and the server sends me to Premium instead. Nothing tells me the house is saved or what would make the site email me. On a second house the wall is fair, but it quotes only £9.99 a month, sends me to a Premium page 17 screens long on my phone, and forgets which house I was paying for.

The tools are fun and stop one step short. The band picker changes the headline but not the table beneath it. The year buttons on comparables hide rows, but the median never moves. The viewing checklist tells me to use it on my phone, and nothing on it can be ticked. I would sign up. I would not yet trust the green tick, and nothing I did today would bring me back tomorrow.

## What would make me pay

I would sign up on the report I care about, and I would not pay for one house. Your own numbers say the same: nobody has ever paid on a first visit, and both subscribers came back across several days with several homes on the go. So the first visit's job is to make a second visit likely, and the second house is where paying should become the obvious step.

Today the path breaks in four places. The page contradicts the free report at the moment it is offered. The PDF promise fails straight after the unlock. Nothing after the unlock sets up a return. The payment moment forgets the house. Fix those first, then add the reasons to come back. This is the path I would follow if each step kept its promise:

1. **Before signing up** Every page states the same offer once, before any lock: 26 checks free with no account, and all 44 on your first home with a free account and no card. The report's all-clear covers only the checks I can see.
2. **The free unlock** It opens with no contradiction, says the home is saved in My properties, and names exactly what would make the site email me: a new sale at this postcode, a new energy certificate or a change of flood zone. Never on a schedule.
3. **Between visits** The home earns a return visit: an alert about something that matters, the viewing checklist I ticked at the viewing with the seller's answers, and the asking price I typed on comparables.
4. **The second house** The wall names both prices, calls the three-month plan the one made for a house hunt, and says how many of the locked checks found something worth checking on this house, as a count, never which.
5. **Paying** The house travels through Premium and checkout, and I land back on it with every check open. My properties shows which of my homes are open in full and which are not.

### The changes, ranked by likely effect on second visits and payments

1. **Stop telling new accounts their free report is used** (Effort S). The report's banner says "You've used your free report" to every signed-in account on a locked home, including a brand-new one with its unlock unused. Show that line only once the unlock is spent, and add a test. Details under High, below.
2. **Keep one PDF promise** (Effort S). Sign-up promises the PDF and the unlocked report shows the button, but the PDF route sends anyone without a subscription to Premium, and Premium and How we compare call it paid. Decide which is true and make all four pages say it.
3. **Say what the unlock gave me and what will bring me back** (Effort S). After the unlock, the "Kept in My properties" line and the first-home extension offer both disappear. Replace the notice with three facts: unlocked and saved; the address alerts go to; exactly what triggers one, never on a schedule. The comparison offer stays with the second home, as shipped on 17 Sep.
4. **Carry the house through to payment** (Effort M). The wall's "See plans" drops the house. Premium then sells again from the top with "You have used all 1 free reports.", and checkout ends on "Back to search". Carry the home in the link, open Premium with a short block for that home, and return to it, open, after checkout.
5. **Make the three-month plan the plain answer** (Effort S). The wall quotes only "£9.99 a month". Name both prices there. Relabel the £24.99 card for a house hunt instead of "SAVE 17% VS MONTHLY". Move "Before you pay", with its answer to "Am I stuck with a monthly bill?", from the end of a 17-screen phone page to just under the prices.
6. **Count what the locks found instead of showing an all-clear** (Effort S). Where a locked check found something, say how many and never which: "1 of the locked checks found something worth checking on this home". It is the most honest reason to unlock or pay, and it replaces a green banner that can vouch for checks nobody has seen.
7. **Put the offer where returning buyers land** (Effort S). Alert emails lead to My properties, which never says which homes are open in full. Label each card, and when two or more are closed, give the price framed around those homes.
8. **Tell the truth about cancelling** (Effort S). Premium's FAQ says everything unlocked stays unlocked after cancelling, but homes opened while subscribed lock again. Correct it, or, as your decision, keep saved homes open for good when a subscription ends, which answers "I only have one house" without the held pass.
9. **Say change alerts are free, everywhere** (Effort S). Premium's FAQ lists change alerts among what Premium adds; the table above it and sign-up say they are free. Alerts are the one message allowed to bring people back, so no page should talk people out of them.
10. **Alert only on changes that matter** (Effort S). Crime is compared one month against the last snapshot and alerts at a difference of 5, without naming the months. Check /admin for how many emails carried only a crime change, keep month-to-month crime on the page, and lead each email with a new sale, a new certificate or a flood change.

## What needs improving, in priority order

### High

**A new account is told "You've used your free report" on the page that offers it** (Report, first view after signing up, desktop and phone; Effort S)

- What I saw: The top of the page asks "You have 1 free full report. Use it on this property?" and a pop-up asks "Use your free full report on M14 5TG?". A scroll lower, the banner says "You've used your free report. The ones you opened stay unlocked for good. Unlocking new ones is £9.99 a month" with "See plans". In the template, the signed-in banner falls back to that wording whenever the home is locked, and never checks whether the unlock is still unused.
- Why it matters: Every account passes this page in the minute after signing up, which is when every recent unlock happened. Promised a free report, then told it is gone beside a price: it reads as bait and switch.
- Suggestion: Show "You've used your free report" only once the unlock is spent. With an unlock left, the one offer on the page is "Yes, unlock every card". Add a test that a new account never sees the phrase.

**The locked findings are in the page for anyone who looks** (Report for a visitor without an account, every device; Effort M)

- What I saw: The live KT3 4HX page, 554 KB, contains the locked answers, hidden only by CSS: "Air Quality 1.7× WHO guideline at worst", "Subsidence Risk Improbable by 2030", "Bus Service 16.5 an hour, weekday daytime", "School Catchment Areas 4 likely, 0 borderline, 4 unlikely", and the valuation popup with "Estimate (median)" and every sale used. The template's own comment says a locked card's finding must not leak.
- Why it matters: View source, a reader mode, a scraper or an AI assistant gets the whole paid report without an account. If people notice, the free unlock and Premium look like theatre.
- Suggestion: When a home is locked, render locked cards as title, icon and one neutral line, and locked popups as their method-and-source paragraph with the sign-up button, with no figures. Make the valuation endpoint refuse locked requests too. This also removes a large share of the report's page weight.

**The PDF promised with the free report is refused** (Sign-up, the unlocked report, Premium and How we compare; Effort S)

- What I saw: Sign-up lists "The full PDF report to keep, print or send" under what you get today. After the free unlock, the report shows "Download full PDF report" and says "The PDF report includes it." The PDF route sends every account without a subscription to Premium. The Premium table says "PDF report to keep or send: Not included", and How we compare puts the PDF on Premium.
- Why it matters: The PDF is what a buyer takes to a partner or a conveyancer. A button that fails straight after the free unlock turns the first taste of the paid product into a broken promise.
- Suggestion: Decide once. Either the free first property includes its PDF, so the route lets an unlocked home through and the table row changes, or the PDF is Premium only, so the sign-up line goes and the button hides on free unlocks. Then make all four pages say the same, with a test.

**Council tax pages and area guides promise an address's own band** (Council tax page (Basildon) and area guide (LS6), then the report; Effort S)

- What I saw: Basildon: "Run a free report for the band an address actually carries, the bill that follows from it". LS6: "the full report gives the band of a specific address". The report shows Band D, and its popup says "ask which band this property is in". The buying guide says reports do not show the band, and /methodology lists it under what the site deliberately does not show.
- Why it matters: Council tax pages are one of the page types Google sends people to. The visitor clicks for the promised figure and is told to ask the seller, before any sign-up offer appears.
- Suggestion: Say what is true: the report shows every band's bill for the council; the band itself is on the seller's bill or free at gov.uk/council-tax-bands; running costs shows the bill for the band you pick. Add a test that no page promises an address's band.

**The school page says "Likely", then the report says it cannot say** (School page after a postcode check and the admissions hub, then the report; Effort S)

- What I saw: Fortismere, checked against N10 3JA, says "Likely", then "Run the full report on N10 3JA for every school at once". The report's free card reads "Likely for 4 schools, borderline 2", but its popup opens "we can't say whether this address falls within any school's admission area", and each school's reading sits behind the locked School Catchment Areas card. The schools guide for a postcode gives every reading free. The count also mixes published distances with modelled estimates without marking which.
- Why it matters: School pages bring the site its best search traffic. The visitor arrives holding a "Likely" and the next page disowns it, at the moment they decide whether to trust the site with an email address.
- Suggestion: Rewrite the popup: Likely means inside the distance the school last admitted from, taken from the council's published figure or a labelled estimate, and never a guarantee. Mark estimated readings "est." on the card, as the schools guide does. Point "every school at once" at the schools guide for that postcode, which delivers it free, or say plainly that each school's reading opens with the free account.

**A postcode search stitches "this property" together from different homes** (Report top and What stands out, any postcode searched without a house number; Effort M)

- What I saw: KT3 4HX: "Semi-detached house · 122 m²" and "£1,867 a year energy" come from the latest certificate, 57 Malden Hill Gardens; "£823,500 last sold here, 2025" and "Freehold" are 55 Malden Hill Gardens. The locked valuation divides one by the other. Running costs for the same postcode says "13 freehold, 16 leasehold of 29 recorded sales". BN1 1EE leads What stands out with "£3,000,000 last sold here", which is 2 to 3 Pavilion Buildings. The house number filter sits below the map.
- Why it matters: A first-time buyer reads the top block as facts about the home they are buying. Tenure alone decides whether they need a lease check, and here the headline says Freehold on a mostly leasehold postcode.
- Suggestion: When no number is given, put "Which home is yours?" under the postcode, with a chip for each address the site already holds from the EPC register and the Land Registry. Until one is chosen, show the postcode's own figures (energy range, tenure split, sales count and middle price), name the home the EPC line describes, and keep "last sold here" out of What stands out.

**On a phone, the answer column is off the edge of the tables that matter** (Admissions hub, schools guide, private schools, comparables and area guide school table, 375px; Effort M)

- What I saw: The Birmingham hub's header is cut to "ADMITTED F", and distances read "0", "1", "2" (0.14, 1.16 and 2.59 miles). The schools guide shows only School, Phase and Ofsted across 16 screens, with the Likely column off screen. Comparables shows Address, Postcode and Type, with price, date and distance off the edge for all 300 rows across 29.5 screens. Nothing says the tables scroll sideways, and the automated overflow check passes because a section scrolls, not the page.
- Why it matters: These are the pages parents and buyers land on from Google, usually on a phone, and each is named for the figure it hides.
- Suggestion: Below about 600px, show each row as a stacked block with the answer first: "0.14 mi, 2023/24"; "Likely: 0.2 mi away, admitted from 0.59 mi"; "£823,500 · 2025-12-08 · 0 yd". Show comparables 25 rows at a time, and fix its caption "most recent first", since the rows are nearest first.

**The all-clear covers checks that were hidden or could not run** (Report banner, for a visitor without an unlock; Effort S)

- What I saw: "No major red flags found across flood, noise, radon, air quality, contamination, planning and area indicators." Air quality and contamination are locked, and the template turns locked flags to clear before building this banner. During the walk the noise service failed ("Data unavailable") and the Coal Authority check failed on all three reports, yet the banner stayed green. On KT3 4HX the locked checks happened to be clear, so today's banner hid nothing, but a locked landfill or mining finding would still show green.
- Why it matters: In a due-diligence report the all-clear is the sentence people act on. It must never vouch for a check the visitor cannot see, or one that did not run.
- Suggestion: Name only what was read: "No red flags in the 26 checks open to you. Noise could not be checked just now." Treat a failed service as not checked. Where a locked check found something, give the count only, as in What would make me pay.

**Nobody is named as running the site** (Every page footer, Privacy, Terms and Premium; Effort S)

- What I saw: The footer offers support@ukpropertyinsight.co.uk and nothing else. Privacy says the site "is operated as a UK-based property due-diligence service". No page names a person, a trading name, a company number or a geographic address, and refunds appear only in the Terms.
- Why it matters: I am about to act on its flood and school answers and then pay £9.99 a month. A site that will not say who runs it looks like a scraper. UK rules for traders selling online also expect a name and a geographic address before a paid contract (the Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002 and the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), which is worth confirming with an adviser.
- Suggestion: Add a "Who runs this" line to the footer and to Premium, with a name or trading name, a UK service address and how quickly support replies. This has waited on your decision since the 16 Sep search audit, because it is your personal information.

**The homepage comparison table undercuts the site's own proof** (Homepage "What buyers do instead", phone and desktop; Effort S)

- What I saw: On a phone, only the row labels and the "Listings portals" column show; the "DIY on gov.uk" and UKPropertyInsight columns are off to the right. On desktop the DIY column says "12+ separate sites" and "Hours", while the site's own timed record says 28 websites and 36 minutes for someone who already knew every site.
- Why it matters: On a phone, the block meant to show why the site beats doing it yourself shows only the portals. On desktop it makes the unmeasured time claim the site has ruled out, two clicks from the measured one.
- Suggestion: Use the measured figures, "28 websites" and "36 minutes, by someone who already knew every site", linked to the record. On phones, stack each row as DIY against UKPropertyInsight and drop the portals column.

### Medium

**The offer is worded four ways, and the FAQ promises what the report does not show** (Homepage hero, offer band, orbit, closing block and FAQ; area guides and hubs; Effort S)

- What I saw: The hero says "Free, and no account needed." The FAQ says "You'll see headline verdicts on every check straight away", yet 18 of 45 cards are locked with no verdict. The offer then reads "normally £9.99/month" struck through, "26 FREE ON EVERY REPORT · 18 MORE WITH PREMIUM, FREE ON YOUR FIRST PROPERTY", and "the first property come with every Premium check unlocked". Area guides and hubs add "Free, no account." under "Run the 44 checks".
- Why it matters: A first-timer takes the hero line as the deal, so the locks a minute later feel like bait. Four wordings of one offer read like terms and conditions.
- Suggestion: Use one sentence, word for word, everywhere: "26 checks free with no account. Sign up free, no card, and your first home gets all 44." Delete "headline verdicts on every check" and fix "come". The struck-through band has waited on your decision since 16 Sep.

**Premium contradicts itself about alerts and about cancelling** (Premium page, its FAQ and its structured data; Effort S)

- What I saw: The FAQ lists "change alerts on saved properties" among what Premium adds; the table above says alerts are included free, and sign-up promises them. The FAQ also says "Cancelling stops the next payment; everything you unlocked stays unlocked", but only the free report is stored as an unlock, so homes opened while subscribed lock again. A free account sees "You have used all 1 free reports.".
- Why it matters: A buyer checking what £24.99 buys gets three answers, is talked out of the free feature that brings people back, and after cancelling finds a promise broken on the page they paid from.
- Suggestion: Keep one list of what each plan includes, and build the table, the FAQ, its structured data, sign-up and How we compare from it. Say alerts are free. Correct the cancelling line, or make it true, as your decision. Fix "all 1 free reports".

**Two counts of "things worth checking" a few centimetres apart** (Report top, YO1 7HH and BN1 1EE; Effort S)

- What I saw: YO1 7HH: the verdict says "balanced against 2 things worth checking", and the line below says "3 things worth checking on this property", adding the council's exceptional financial support. BN1 1EE shows 3 against 4. "Section 114 notice" is not explained on the chip.
- Why it matters: Two numbers for one thing make me doubt both.
- Suggestion: Build both from one list, and say the fact on the chip in plain words.

**"Lower crime" on a difference of one** (Report verdict, What stands out and the crime card; Effort S)

- What I saw: "Lower crime than the surrounding area" rests on "229 crimes recorded within ~1 mile in 2026-07, versus 230 in the wider KT3 postcode area". Central York reads 2 against 2. BN1 1EE shows "1834" without a comma.
- Why it matters: It adds to "88/100 EXCELLENT" on a difference of one crime in one month, and the popup shows as much. One overstated claim makes a reader discount the rest.
- Suggestion: Say lower or higher only when the totals differ by a stated margin, and "about the same" otherwise. Put the month and radius on the card. When counts are in single figures, say Police.uk holds few records here that month.

**The locked price trend draws a fall beside "prices rising"** (Locked Price Trend and Forecast card, KT3 4HX; Effort S)

- What I saw: The popup lists "Now £591,555", then "Projected, +1 year £579,490", from a straight line whose starting point sits about £12,000 under the latest index, while the free page says "+3.3% house prices year on year" and "Area prices rising".
- Why it matters: Someone who unlocks it gets an alarming figure that contradicts the official index on the same page, from a projection rather than a published figure.
- Suggestion: Drop the projection, rename the card "Price trend", and keep the House Price Index line with its 1, 5 and 10 year changes.

**The questions to ask are few, generic and last** (Report section "Before you offer"; Effort M)

- What I saw: KT3 4HX says "4 questions were generated for this property", three of them fixed for every purchase. Findings the page itself flags produce none: YO1 7HH's council under exceptional support and listed buildings 9 yards away, Likely school readings, an EPC D with £8,400 to £12,200 to reach C. The section comes after all six groups.
- Why it matters: The list a buyer sends to the agent, the seller and the solicitor is what turns 44 facts into an answer, and it is what people keep.
- Suggestion: Add questions from findings already on the page: a listed building within about 25 m, a Likely school, an EPC of D to G with its cost to reach C, a check that could not run. Show those from free cards in full to everyone. Move the section up under the banner, and feed the viewing checklist from the same list.

**Locked cards are blank, some are free next door, and two open to nothing** (Report groups; Effort M)

- What I saw: 18 locked tiles show a title and a padlock, and a tap goes straight to sign-up. Rental analysis, household income and stamp duty are locked here and free on the running-costs page the report links at the top. On a postcode search, "Extended or Modified" unlocks to "Search with a house number" and "Mining Risk" to "Data unavailable".
- Why it matters: Blank tiles read as a wall, and finding the locked rent free one click later makes the lock feel arbitrary.
- Suggestion: Give each locked card one neutral line saying what it answers and who publishes it (waiting on your decision since 16 Sep). Decide the split: lock less on the report, or give less on running costs. Say "needs a house number" before sign-up, and say a failed check could not be checked.

**On a phone the verdict breaks apart and the answer arrives on screen three** (Report top, 375px; Effort S)

- What I saw: The verdict renders as centred, underlined fragments with lone semicolons. Then running costs, the property line and a screen-tall map come before "No major red flags" and the house number filter.
- Why it matters: The first phone screen is where an anxious buyer decides whether the site is credible.
- Suggestion: Let the reasons wrap inline or as a short list. Order the top as grade, reasons, banner, "Which home is yours?", then costs, and load the map on a tap on phones, in both map branches.

**The wait page has no ending** (Wait page for a cold report; Effort S)

- What I saw: The page asks every 0.9 seconds with no limit. Once every source is in, it says "All sources back, putting the report together" and never changes. One path cannot finish: a result over the cache's 4 MB entry limit is never stored, so the page would restart the fetch every 30 seconds. Normal reports are 1 to 2.5 MB, so this was not seen today, and two visitors on the same address cannot stall each other.
- Why it matters: A buyer who watches a full dial that never opens a report will not search again.
- Suggestion: Exempt the report from the entry limit, or record a finished flag. After 30 seconds, name what is still missing; after 60, offer "Try again" and the area guide.

**Comparables takes over 2 seconds on every visit** (/property/comparables; Effort S)

- What I saw: Live, comparables for KT3 4HX took 2.61 s and then 2.26 s to first byte on consecutive requests, against about 0.4 to 0.6 s for the report beside it. The route fetches nearby sales on every request without a cache, although the report has already fetched the same data.
- Why it matters: It is where people land after tapping "What sold nearby", just as they get interested.
- Suggestion: Cache it by coordinates, or serve it from the report's own fetch.

**The count of sources is 13, 19 or 14, depending on the page** (Homepage, wait page, /methodology and How we compare; Effort S)

- What I saw: The homepage says "13 official sources", a hand-typed number that counts OpenStreetMap. The wait page counts "0 of 19 sources back". /methodology lists 14 bodies and leaves out the Coal Authority, NHS England, the Bus Open Data Service and others that Premium names. How we compare sets our checks beside a rival's "14 official data sources".
- Why it matters: A careful first-timer checks the numbers, and three counts on the pages that make the case for trust look careless.
- Suggestion: Keep one list of publishing bodies in code, as the check count already is, pinned by a test. Call the wait page's 19 "lookups".

**"Why trust this?" contradicts the report** (/methodology and /areas; Effort S)

- What I saw: "What we deliberately don't show" lists brownfield land, which Premium lists as a check and the wait page ticks off. It calls the council tax band list a paid product, while the by-hand record times a "VOA band lookup" at 1.3 minutes. /areas promises every district a flood zone and "Band D council tax from MHCLG", but flood zones cover England only, and Band D comes from three governments, with none for Northern Ireland.
- Why it matters: The people who open "Why trust this?" are the sceptics, and a page that contradicts the report loses them.
- Suggestion: Take brownfield off the not-shown list. Give the band one reason that agrees with the record, your call which is true. Word /areas for what each nation actually has.

**The market report calls counties cities, and Nottingham reports use the county** (/market-report and reports for Nottingham addresses; Effort S)

- What I saw: "18 major UK cities" includes "Nottinghamshire" and "Aberdeenshire", because the area match prefers the shortest label that contains the name. An NG1 report would quote Nottinghamshire's average, labelled as such. The title says "17 September 2026" over July figures, and "Steepest falling: City of Westminster (-20.7%)" carries no caveat.
- Why it matters: A county average labelled as a city is a wrong figure beside an official source's name.
- Suggestion: Prefer a "City of" label after an exact match, with Nottingham and Aberdeen as test cases. Name the data month in the title. Add a line on thin samples beside large moves.

**School wording that reads like machine output, and a hub that hides its gaps** (School page verdict and the Birmingham admissions hub; Effort S)

- What I saw: The verdict reads "N10 3JA is 0.4 miles from Fortismere School. comfortably inside the distance the school admitted from last time (1.883 miles ...)", with a lower-case start and three decimals beside a "1.88 mi" tile. The Birmingham hub lists 52 primary schools from 2023/24 and never says secondary schools are missing. Its "Sign up to save the schools you care about" returns a new account to rows with nothing to save.
- Why it matters: This is the answer the most-visited page type exists to give, and the hub's gap sends parents of older children away with nothing.
- Suggestion: A capital letter and two decimals. A line saying which phase and year the hub holds. A save button on each hub row for signed-in accounts, carrying the school through sign-up.

**Dead ends between content pages and the report** (Schools guide, private schools, hubs, council tax, market report and buying guide; Effort S)

- What I saw: The schools guide for KT3 4HX has no link to the KT3 4HX report and no save or share. "full property report" on private schools and hubs, "Run a free report" on council tax and "search it directly" on the market report all go to the homepage. ## How the site could be more interactive

There is already plenty to play with: group tiles, verdict reasons that open their cards, the band picker, year buttons on comparables, a second district on area guides, an extra area on the schools guide, the school typeahead, postcode checks and neighbouring rings on school pages, and the lamp. Most of these stop one step short, and nothing I do is kept, so nothing pulls me back.

Each idea below uses data the site already holds, keeps something for the buyer, and is ranked by usefulness against effort. Free ones pull people in; the Premium one gives a reason to pay that grows with every house.

1. **Is the asking price in line? On comparables** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: Type chips (Detached, Semi, Terraced, Flat, with Land Registry's "Other" off by default) and a tenure toggle recalculate the median, the range and the bar together with the year buttons, which today hide rows but never move the figures. Among the 80 semi-detached sales alone, £823,500 sits above 40%, not the 71% the page shows against every sale. An asking price box places the price on the bar in one sentence, for example "£850,000 is above 6 of the 22 semi-detached sales here in the last 2 years", and shows stamp duty for a mover, a first-time buyer and an additional home.
   - Data it uses: HM Land Registry Price Paid rows already on the page (type, tenure, price, date, distance); the stamp duty bands in the site's own calculator
   - Reason to return or pay: Every house has an asking price. Kept with a saved home, it is the most natural reason to save and reopen, and it gives the Premium comparison the buyer's own numbers. It ranks recorded sales and never estimates a price.
2. **A viewing checklist you can tick** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: Real checkboxes, each with a line for what the seller or agent said, saved on the device as you tick ("7 of 12 checked, 2 to follow up"). Signed in, the ticks and answers go onto the home in My properties and into the side-by-side comparison. Printing still works.
   - Data it uses: The report's own findings, through the existing viewing checklist
   - Reason to return or pay: The viewing is the buyer's most engaged moment and the step before a second visit. A ticked list with answers is what they reopen that evening and hold against the next house.
3. **Homes you looked at, on every school page** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: Under the postcode checker, every address you opened on this device, or saved when signed in, with its distance and its Likely, Borderline or Unlikely reading, each linked to its report. On the school shortlist, a grid of saved homes against shortlisted schools, from published figures only.
   - Data it uses: Council-published admission distances, DfE school coordinates, the device's recent places and My properties
   - Reason to return or pay: Google sends parents to school pages. The answer for all their houses at once, kept across devices with an account, is a reason to sign up on the very page they arrived on.
4. **A band picker that updates everything** (Free; Effort S)
   - What you do and get: Picking Band F updates both table rows, not only the headline, goes into the share link, and stays with a saved home. A toggle swaps today's energy cost for the cost after the certificate's recommended measures (£974 against £671 for KT3 4HX).
   - Data it uses: Council band tables and EPC figures already on the page
   - Reason to return or pay: Today the page shows two different yearly totals for one house one screen apart, and shares the wrong one with a partner.
5. **Several years of admission distances** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: Where a council publishes several years (Haringey, Bristol, Bexley and Solihull), keep every year, add a year stepper that redraws the ring, and let the checker answer "Likely in 3 of the last 4 published years". The nearby table shows a year instead of "varies".
   - Data it uses: The multi-year council tables the importer already reads and then cuts to one year
   - Reason to return or pay: The page tells parents the distance moves every year and shows one year. Saved schools already alert when a council republishes; this gives those alerts something to compare.
6. **Where both schools are in reach** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: Add a second school on a school page, with an optional budget: the districts inside both published distances, cheapest first by median sold price, both rings on the map, and a postcode check that answers for both.
   - Data it uses: Published admission distances, DfE coordinates, and district medians from the area guides
   - Reason to return or pay: It answers "where should we look?" before a house exists, and saving the pair shortlists both schools, which alerts when either council republishes.
7. **My must-haves, checked on every report** (Premium; Effort L)
   - What you do and get: Pick up to six conditions from checks the report already rates, such as flood zone, a named school being Likely, EPC band, broadband, buses an hour, a Band D ceiling or tenure. Every report then opens with "4 of 5 met", each line opening its card, and where a source does not cover the address it says so. My properties sorts by must-haves met. It appears only after conditions are set, so it adds no ask before the first card.
   - Data it uses: Card statuses the report already computes from the Environment Agency, the British Geological Survey, council admission distances, the EPC register, Ofcom, the Department for Transport and MHCLG
   - Reason to return or pay: Its value grows with the number of homes, which is how both subscribers used the site. Conditions on free checks stay free; conditions on locked checks are a paywall the buyer chose.
8. **Where the crime is** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: A crime layer on the report map for the month shown, with chips for burglary, vehicle crime, antisocial behaviour and violent crime, and Police.uk's caveat that points are moved to a nearby street.
   - Data it uses: Police.uk street-level records the report already fetches and then reduces to counts
   - Reason to return or pay: Where incidents cluster is what people look at and send to a partner, and each monthly release is a reason to look again.
9. **One box for a postcode, district, town, council or school** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: The homepage box takes "LS6", "Kingston" or "Tiffin School", with suggestions grouped as area guide, council tax or school. A full postcode still runs the report. A district already redirects today, but nothing on the homepage or /areas says so.
   - Data it uses: The districts list, the council tax councils and the school search already on the site
   - Reason to return or pay: Many buyers start with a place or a school, which is exactly what Google sends them to, and today the homepage cannot take it.
10. **Private schools near a house, for a child's age** (Free; Effort M)
   - What you do and get: A postcode box and age chips ("places at 4", "at 11", "at 16") on a council's private schools page, linked to the schools guide, which already gives fee-paying schools' distance from a postcode.
   - Data it uses: DfE register age ranges, coordinates and pupils on roll
   - Reason to return or pay: It turns a list read once into a tool used against each house viewed.
11. **District comparisons that show what differs** (Free; Effort S)
   - What you do and get: When two districts share a council, fold the identical council rows into one line and add the district rows the guide already holds (deprivation, buses an hour, GP list size, share renting), with the "only what differs" toggle the full comparison page already has.
   - Data it uses: Area guide figures from MHCLG, the Department for Transport, NHS England, ONS and the Land Registry
   - Reason to return or pay: Neighbours are the comparison people actually make, and today half the rows are identical.

What not to add stays as you set it: no confetti, sounds, auto-sliding carousels, chatbot or estimated-value dial, and no count-up beyond the trust figures you asked for on 12 Sep. Every idea above ranks or checks recorded facts, and none of them estimates a price.

## 初次訪客的感受

在手機上，五秒內我就知道這是甚麼網站：「Prices, schools, and the running costs no listing shows you」，一句清楚說明各項檢查來自發佈數據的機構，還有一個郵區輸入框。幾乎每句都註明來源，還有一個公開記錄承認曾經出錯。這份誠實是網站最好的地方。在手提電腦上，一張示範報告卡放在標題上面，把輸入框推到畫面底邊，點它也沒有反應。

冷報告的等待做得好：「Usually 5 to 15 seconds」下面，一列真實來源逐項打勾，我的報告 10.4 秒完成。報告以「88/100 EXCELLENT」和「No major red flags found」開頭。然後疑問開始出現。這間半獨立屋、它每年 £1,867 的能源費和 £823,500 的成交價，其實來自不同的鄰居，因為我只輸入了郵區、沒有門牌號碼。空氣質素和污染都被鎖上，綠色橫額卻替它們擔保。被鎖的方格是空白的，而它們隱藏的租金、收入和印花稅，在下一頁卻是免費的。要問賣家的問題放在最底，四條之中三條適用於任何房子。

## 甚麼會令我付費

我會為自己在意的那份報告註冊，但不會為一間房子付費。你自己的數字也是這樣說：從來沒有人在第一次到訪時付費，而兩位訂閱者都是在幾天內多次回來、同時在看幾間房子的人。所以第一次到訪的任務是令第二次到訪更有可能，而第二間房子才是付費應該變得理所當然的時刻。

現時這條路在四個地方斷掉。頁面在提供免費報告的那一刻自相矛盾。PDF 的承諾在解鎖後立刻落空。解鎖之後沒有任何東西安排回訪。付款那一刻忘了是哪間房子。先修好這四點，再加入回來的理由。如果每一步都守住承諾，我會這樣走：

1. **註冊之前** 每一頁都在任何鎖之前，用同一句話說明一次優惠：不用帳戶有 26 項免費檢查，開免費帳戶、不用信用卡，第一間房子就有全部 44 項。報告的「無問題」只涵蓋我看得到的檢查。
2. **免費解鎖** 打開時不再自相矛盾，說明房子已存入「我的物業」，並清楚列出甚麼情況會發電郵給我：這個郵區有新成交、有新的能源證書，或者水浸區域改變。絕不定期發送。
3. **兩次到訪之間** 房子贏得回訪：一個關於重要事情的提示、我在看樓時打勾並記下賣家答案的清單，以及我在成交比較輸入的叫價。
4. **第二間房子** 付費牆列出兩個價錢，說明三個月計劃是為找樓而設，並告訴我被鎖的檢查中有多少項在這間房子發現值得留意的事，只給數目，不說是哪一項。
5. **付款** 房子跟著我經過 Premium 和結帳，付款後我回到它的報告，所有檢查都已打開。「我的物業」顯示我哪幾間房子已完整打開、哪幾間還沒有。

### 改動，按對回訪和付費的可能影響排序

1. **不要再告訴新帳戶免費報告已用完** (工作量 S). 報告的橫額對每個已登入、而房子仍鎖著的帳戶都說「You've used your free report」，包括解鎖還未用的新帳戶。只在解鎖已用後才顯示這句，並加上測試。詳情見下文「高」。
2. **PDF 只作一個承諾** (工作量 S). 註冊頁承諾 PDF，解鎖後的報告也顯示按鈕，但 PDF 路由把所有沒有訂閱的人送到 Premium，而 Premium 頁和比較頁都說 PDF 要付費。決定哪個才是真的，讓四個頁面說同一件事。
3. **說清楚解鎖給了我甚麼、甚麼會令我回來** (工作量 S). 解鎖後，「Kept in My properties」那句和第一間房子的擴充功能推介都一併消失。把通知換成三件事：已解鎖並已儲存；提示會發到哪個電郵；甚麼情況會觸發提示，絕不定期發送。比較功能的推介按 9 月 17 日的做法留給第二間房子。
4. **讓房子一直跟到付款** (工作量 M). 付費牆的「See plans」丟失了房子。Premium 頁又從頭推銷，還寫著「You have used all 1 free reports.」，結帳後停在「Back to search」。在連結中帶上房子，Premium 頁為那間房子開一個簡短區塊，結帳後回到那間已打開的房子。
5. **讓三個月計劃成為直接的答案** (工作量 S). 付費牆只報「£9.99 a month」。在那裡列出兩個價錢。把 £24.99 的卡片改為「為找樓而設」，取代「SAVE 17% VS MONTHLY」。把「Before you pay」（包括「Am I stuck with a monthly bill?」的答案）從手機上 17 個畫面長的頁面最尾，移到價錢下面。
6. **用被鎖檢查的發現數目，取代「全無問題」** (工作量 S). 如果被鎖的檢查發現了甚麼，只說有多少項，不說是哪一項：「1 of the locked checks found something worth checking on this home」。這是解鎖或付費最誠實的理由，並取代那個可以替沒人看過的檢查擔保的綠色橫額。
7. **把優惠放在回訪買家到達的地方** (工作量 S). 提示電郵會帶人到「我的物業」，但那裡從不說明哪幾間房子已完整打開。為每張卡片加標籤；當有兩間或以上仍鎖著，就以這幾間房子為前提說出價錢。
8. **如實說明取消訂閱的後果** (工作量 S). Premium 的常見問題說取消後所有已解鎖的都保持解鎖，但訂閱期間打開的房子會再次鎖上。改正這句；或者由你決定，在訂閱結束時讓已儲存的房子永久保持打開，這樣不用暫緩中的單次通行證也能回答「我只有一間房子」。
9. **各處都說明變動提示是免費的** (工作量 S). Premium 的常見問題把變動提示列為 Premium 附加功能，上面的表格和註冊頁卻說是免費的。提示是唯一獲准帶人回來的訊息，任何頁面都不應勸人不要用它。
10. **只在重要的變動時提示** (工作量 S). 罪案是拿一個月和上次記錄比較，相差 5 宗就發提示，而且不寫是哪個月。先在 /admin 查看有多少封電郵只有罪案變動；每月罪案數字留在頁面上，電郵則以新成交、新能源證書或水浸區域改變開頭。

## 需要改進的地方，按優先次序

### 高

**新帳戶在提供免費報告的同一頁被告知「You've used your free report」** (報告，註冊後第一次瀏覽，電腦及手機; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 頁面頂部問「You have 1 free full report. Use it on this property?」，彈窗問「Use your free full report on M14 5TG?」。往下捲一點，橫額卻寫「You've used your free report. The ones you opened stay unlocked for good. Unlocking new ones is £9.99 a month」並附「See plans」。模板中，已登入的橫額只要房子仍鎖著就會顯示這句，從不檢查解鎖是否仍未使用。
- 為何重要: 每個帳戶在註冊後的一分鐘內都會經過這頁，而近期所有解鎖都在這一分鐘發生。先承諾免費報告，再說已用完並附上價錢，看起來就是貨不對辦。
- 建議: 只在解鎖已用後才顯示「You've used your free report」。仍有解鎖時，頁面上唯一的優惠是「Yes, unlock every card」。加上測試，確保新帳戶永遠看不到這句。

**被鎖的結果就在網頁原始碼裡，誰都看得到** (沒有帳戶的訪客看到的報告，所有裝置; 工作量 M)

- 我看到的: 正式網站上 KT3 4HX 的頁面（554 KB）包含被鎖的答案，只是用 CSS 隱藏：「Air Quality 1.7× WHO guideline at worst」、「Subsidence Risk Improbable by 2030」、「Bus Service 16.5 an hour, weekday daytime」、「School Catchment Areas 4 likely, 0 borderline, 4 unlikely」，還有附「Estimate (median)」及所有參考成交的估值彈窗。模板自己的註解也寫明，被鎖卡片的結果不可外洩。
- 為何重要: 查看原始碼、閱讀模式、爬蟲或 AI 助手，不用帳戶就能拿到整份付費報告。一旦有人發現，免費解鎖和 Premium 就像做戲。
- 建議: 房子鎖著時，被鎖卡片只顯示標題、圖示和一句中性說明；被鎖彈窗只顯示方法和來源段落及註冊按鈕，不含任何數字。估值接口也要拒絕被鎖的請求。這樣亦會大幅減輕報告頁面的重量。

**隨免費報告承諾的 PDF 被拒絕** (註冊頁、解鎖後的報告、Premium 頁和比較頁; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 註冊頁在「今天可得到」下列出「The full PDF report to keep, print or send」。免費解鎖後，報告顯示「Download full PDF report」，並寫著「The PDF report includes it.」。PDF 路由把所有沒有訂閱的帳戶送到 Premium。Premium 表格寫「PDF report to keep or send: Not included」，比較頁則把 PDF 列為 Premium 功能。
- 為何重要: PDF 是買家拿給伴侶或律師看的東西。按鈕在免費解鎖後立刻失效，令付費產品的第一次體驗變成違背承諾。
- 建議: 一次過決定。要麼免費的第一間房子包括 PDF（讓已解鎖的房子通過路由，並修改表格），要麼 PDF 只屬 Premium（刪除註冊頁那句，並在免費解鎖時隱藏按鈕）。然後讓四個頁面說同一件事，並加上測試。

**市政稅頁和地區指南承諾顯示個別地址的稅階** (市政稅頁（Basildon）及地區指南（LS6），然後是報告; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: Basildon：「Run a free report for the band an address actually carries, the bill that follows from it」。LS6：「the full report gives the band of a specific address」。報告只顯示 Band D，彈窗寫「ask which band this property is in」。置業指南說報告不顯示稅階，/methodology 也把它列在網站刻意不顯示的項目中。
- 為何重要: 市政稅頁是 Google 帶人來的頁面類型之一。訪客為了承諾的數字點進來，卻在任何註冊優惠出現之前就被叫去問賣家。
- 建議: 如實說明：報告顯示該議會每個稅階的稅單；稅階本身在賣家的稅單上，或可在 gov.uk/council-tax-bands 免費查到；生活開支頁會顯示你所選稅階的稅單。加上測試，確保沒有頁面承諾顯示個別地址的稅階。

**學校頁說「Likely」，報告卻說無法判斷** (學校頁輸入郵區後及入學距離總覽，然後是報告; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: Fortismere 用 N10 3JA 檢查後顯示「Likely」，再寫「Run the full report on N10 3JA for every school at once」。報告的免費卡片寫「Likely for 4 schools, borderline 2」，但彈窗開頭卻是「we can't say whether this address falls within any school's admission area」，而每間學校的判斷都在被鎖的 School Catchment Areas 卡片後面。郵區的學校指南則免費給出所有判斷。這個數目還把公佈的距離和模型估算混在一起，沒有標明哪個是哪個。
- 為何重要: 學校頁為網站帶來最好的搜尋流量。訪客帶著一個「Likely」來到，下一頁卻否認它，正值他們決定是否把電郵交給網站的時刻。
- 建議: 重寫彈窗：Likely 表示在學校上次錄取的距離之內，數字來自議會公佈的數據或已標明的估算，而且從不保證。像學校指南一樣，在卡片上把估算的判斷標為「est.」。把「every school at once」連到該郵區的學校指南（那裡免費提供），或者清楚說明每間學校的判斷要開免費帳戶才看到。

**只搜郵區時，「這間物業」是由不同房子拼湊出來的** (報告頂部及 What stands out，任何沒有門牌號碼的郵區搜尋; 工作量 M)

- 我看到的: KT3 4HX：「Semi-detached house · 122 m²」和「£1,867 a year energy」來自最新的能源證書，即 57 Malden Hill Gardens；「£823,500 last sold here, 2025」和「Freehold」卻是 55 Malden Hill Gardens。被鎖的估值還把兩者相除。同一郵區的生活開支頁寫「13 freehold, 16 leasehold of 29 recorded sales」。BN1 1EE 的 What stands out 以「£3,000,000 last sold here」開頭，那其實是 2 至 3 Pavilion Buildings。門牌號碼篩選放在地圖下面。
- 為何重要: 初次買家會把頂部區塊當作自己要買的那間房子的事實。單是業權形式就決定他們是否需要檢查租約，而這裡在一個大部分是租賃業權的郵區標示「Freehold」。
- 建議: 沒有門牌號碼時，在郵區下面加「Which home is yours?」，為網站已從能源證書登記和土地註冊處取得的每個地址顯示一個選項。選定之前，顯示郵區本身的數字（能源費範圍、業權比例、成交數目和中位價），說明能源證書那行描述的是哪間房子，並且不在 What stands out 顯示「last sold here」。

**在手機上，重要表格的答案欄位在畫面邊緣之外** (入學距離總覽、學校指南、私立學校、成交比較及地區指南的學校表格，375px; 工作量 M)

- 我看到的: Birmingham 總覽的表頭被切成「ADMITTED F」，距離顯示為「0」、「1」、「2」（實為 0.14、1.16 及 2.59 英里）。學校指南在 16 個畫面中只顯示 School、Phase 和 Ofsted，Likely 欄在畫面外。成交比較只顯示地址、郵區和類型，300 行的價錢、日期和距離全在邊緣之外，長達 29.5 個畫面。沒有任何提示說表格可以橫向捲動，而自動溢出檢查會通過，因為捲動的是區塊，不是整頁。
- 為何重要: 這些是家長和買家從 Google 來到的頁面，通常用手機，而每一頁都以它隱藏的那個數字命名。
- 建議: 在約 600px 以下，每行改為疊起的區塊，答案放第一行：「0.14 mi, 2023/24」；「Likely: 0.2 mi away, admitted from 0.59 mi」；「£823,500 · 2025-12-08 · 0 yd」。成交比較每次顯示 25 行，並修正說明文字「most recent first」，因為實際是按距離由近至遠排列。

**「全無問題」涵蓋了被隱藏或未能執行的檢查** (報告橫額，未解鎖的訪客; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 「No major red flags found across flood, noise, radon, air quality, contamination, planning and area indicators.」空氣質素和污染都被鎖上，而模板在組成這句橫額之前，把被鎖的警示改成無問題。走訪期間，噪音服務失敗（「Data unavailable」），煤炭管理局的檢查在三份報告都失敗，橫額仍然是綠色。KT3 4HX 被鎖的檢查剛好都沒有問題，所以今天的橫額沒有隱瞞甚麼，但如果被鎖的堆填區或採礦檢查有發現，它仍會顯示綠色。
- 為何重要: 在盡職調查報告中，「全無問題」是人們據以行事的一句。它絕不可以替訪客看不到、或根本沒有執行的檢查擔保。
- 建議: 只說已讀取的：「No red flags in the 26 checks open to you. Noise could not be checked just now.」服務失敗就當作未檢查。被鎖的檢查有發現時，只給數目，做法見「甚麼會令我付費」。

**沒有說明誰營運這個網站** (每頁頁尾、私隱政策、條款及 Premium 頁; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 頁尾只有 support@ukpropertyinsight.co.uk。私隱政策說網站「is operated as a UK-based property due-diligence service」。沒有任何頁面寫出個人姓名、商號、公司編號或實際地址，退款只在條款中提及。
- 為何重要: 我準備根據它的水浸和學校答案作決定，之後還要每月付 £9.99。一個不肯說誰在營運的網站，看起來像爬取數據的網站。英國對網上賣家的規定亦要求在付費合約前提供名稱和實際地址（2002 年電子商務規例及 2013 年消費者合約規例），值得向顧問確認。
- 建議: 在頁尾和 Premium 頁加一行「Who runs this」：姓名或商號、英國服務地址，以及客服多快回覆。這一點自 9 月 16 日的搜尋審核起一直等你決定，因為涉及你的個人資料。

**首頁的比較表削弱了網站自己的證據** (首頁「What buyers do instead」，手機及電腦; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 在手機上，只看到各行標題和「Listings portals」一欄，「DIY on gov.uk」和 UKPropertyInsight 兩欄在右邊畫面外。在電腦上，DIY 一欄寫「12+ separate sites」和「Hours」，但網站自己的計時記錄是 28 個網站、由一位已熟悉每個網站的人用 36 分鐘。
- 為何重要: 在手機上，這個本應說明網站比自己動手更好的區塊，只顯示了樓盤網站。在電腦上，它作出網站已決定不用的未經量度的時間聲稱，而量度過的記錄就在兩下點擊之外。
- 建議: 改用量度過的數字：「28 websites」和「36 minutes, by someone who already knew every site」，並連到記錄。在手機上，把每行疊成 DIY 對 UKPropertyInsight，刪去樓盤網站一欄。

### 中

**優惠有四種寫法，常見問題還承諾了報告沒有顯示的東西** (首頁標題、優惠橫條、環繞區、結尾區塊及常見問題；地區指南及總覽; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 標題寫「Free, and no account needed.」。常見問題寫「You'll see headline verdicts on every check straight away」，但 45 張卡片中有 18 張鎖上，沒有任何結論。優惠又寫成劃線的「normally £9.99/month」、「26 FREE ON EVERY REPORT · 18 MORE WITH PREMIUM, FREE ON YOUR FIRST PROPERTY」，以及「the first property come with every Premium check unlocked」。地區指南和總覽還在「Run the 44 checks」下加上「Free, no account.」。
- 為何重要: 初次訪客會把標題那句當作交易條件，所以一分鐘後看到的鎖就像誘餌。同一個優惠有四種寫法，讀起來像條款細則。
- 建議: 全站逐字使用同一句：「26 checks free with no account. Sign up free, no card, and your first home gets all 44.」刪去「headline verdicts on every check」，並把「come」改為「comes」。劃線價格的橫條自 9 月 16 日起一直等你決定。

**Premium 頁在提示和取消訂閱兩件事上自相矛盾** (Premium 頁、常見問題及其結構化數據; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 常見問題把「change alerts on saved properties」列為 Premium 附加功能；上面的表格說提示是免費的，註冊頁也承諾提供。常見問題又說「Cancelling stops the next payment; everything you unlocked stays unlocked」，但只有免費報告會記錄為解鎖，所以訂閱期間打開的房子會再次鎖上。免費帳戶還會看到「You have used all 1 free reports.」。
- 為何重要: 想知道 £24.99 買到甚麼的買家會得到三個答案，被勸離那個會帶人回來的免費功能，取消後又發現付款那頁的承諾沒有兌現。
- 建議: 為每個計劃包括的內容保留一份清單，表格、常見問題、結構化數據、註冊頁和比較頁都由它生成。說明提示是免費的。改正取消訂閱那句，或者由你決定令它成真。修正「all 1 free reports」。

**相隔幾厘米的兩個「值得檢查」數目** (報告頂部，YO1 7HH 及 BN1 1EE; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: YO1 7HH：結論寫「balanced against 2 things worth checking」，下一行卻寫「3 things worth checking on this property」，多了議會的特殊財政支援。BN1 1EE 則是 3 對 4。標籤上沒有解釋「Section 114 notice」。
- 為何重要: 同一件事有兩個數字，令人兩個都懷疑。
- 建議: 兩者由同一份清單生成，並在標籤上用淺白的話說明事實。

**只差一宗就說「罪案較低」** (報告結論、What stands out 及罪案卡片; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 「Lower crime than the surrounding area」的根據是「229 crimes recorded within ~1 mile in 2026-07, versus 230 in the wider KT3 postcode area」。約克市中心是 2 對 2。BN1 1EE 顯示「1834」，沒有千位逗號。
- 為何重要: 它憑一個月內相差一宗罪案，為「88/100 EXCELLENT」加分，而彈窗正好顯示出這一點。一個誇大的說法會令讀者對其餘內容打折扣。
- 建議: 只在總數相差達到明確幅度時才說較低或較高，否則說「about the same」。在卡片上寫明月份和範圍。數字只有個位數時，說明 Police.uk 那個月在這裡的記錄很少。

**被鎖的樓價走勢在「樓價上升」旁邊畫出下跌** (被鎖的 Price Trend and Forecast 卡片，KT3 4HX; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 彈窗列出「Now £591,555」，然後是「Projected, +1 year £579,490」，這條直線的起點比最新指數低約 £12,000；而免費頁面卻寫「+3.3% house prices year on year」和「Area prices rising」。
- 為何重要: 解鎖的人會得到一個令人擔心的數字，與同一頁的官方指數相反，而且來自推算，不是公佈的數字。
- 建議: 刪去推算，把卡片改名為「Price trend」，保留房價指數走勢及其 1 年、5 年和 10 年的變化。

**要問的問題太少、太籠統，而且放在最後** (報告區塊「Before you offer」; 工作量 M)

- 我看到的: KT3 4HX 寫「4 questions were generated for this property」，其中三條是每宗買賣都一樣的固定問題。頁面自己標示的發現卻沒有產生任何問題：YO1 7HH 議會接受特殊支援、9 碼外的登錄建築、Likely 的學校判斷、能源評級 D 要花 £8,400 至 £12,200 才達到 C。這個區塊放在六個分組之後。
- 為何重要: 買家發給代理、賣家和律師的問題清單，才是把 44 項事實變成答案的東西，也是人們會保留的東西。
- 建議: 由頁面已有的發現產生問題：約 25 米內有登錄建築、Likely 的學校、能源評級 D 至 G 及其達到 C 的費用、未能執行的檢查。由免費卡片產生的問題向所有人完整顯示。把區塊移到橫額下面，並讓看樓清單使用同一份問題。

**被鎖的卡片是空白的，有些在隔壁頁免費，還有兩張解鎖後甚麼也沒有** (報告分組; 工作量 M)

- 我看到的: 18 個被鎖方格只有標題和鎖頭，一按就直接去註冊。租金分析、家庭收入和印花稅在這裡被鎖，但在報告頂部連到的生活開支頁卻是免費的。只搜郵區時，「Extended or Modified」解鎖後是「Search with a house number」，「Mining Risk」是「Data unavailable」。
- 為何重要: 空白方格看起來像一堵牆；在下一頁免費看到被鎖的租金，令鎖顯得隨意。
- 建議: 每張被鎖卡片加一句中性說明，寫明它回答甚麼、由誰發佈（自 9 月 16 日起等你決定）。決定劃分：報告少鎖一點，或生活開支頁少給一點。在註冊前註明「needs a house number」，並說明失敗的檢查未能完成。

**在手機上，結論句被拆散，答案要到第三個畫面才出現** (報告頂部，375px; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 結論顯示為置中、加底線的碎片，分號獨佔一行。然後是生活開支、物業資料和一整個畫面高的地圖，之後才到「No major red flags」和門牌號碼篩選。
- 為何重要: 焦慮的買家在手機的第一個畫面就決定網站是否可信。
- 建議: 讓理由在同一行換行，或改為短列表。頂部次序改為：評級、理由、橫額、「Which home is yours?」，然後是開支；在手機上把地圖改為按下才載入，兩套地圖都要改。

**等待頁沒有結局** (冷報告的等待頁; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 頁面每 0.9 秒查詢一次，沒有上限。所有來源都回來後，它寫「All sources back, putting the report together」，之後不再改變。有一條路徑永遠完成不了：超過快取每項 4 MB 上限的結果不會被儲存，頁面會每 30 秒重新抓取一次。正常報告是 1 至 2.5 MB，所以今天沒有遇到；兩位訪客同時查同一地址也不會互相卡住。
- 為何重要: 看著轉盤滿格卻始終打不開報告的買家，不會再搜尋。
- 建議: 讓報告不受每項大小上限限制，或記錄一個完成標記。30 秒後說明還在等哪些來源；60 秒後提供「Try again」及地區指南。

**成交比較每次都要超過 2 秒** (/property/comparables; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 在正式網站上，KT3 4HX 的成交比較連續兩次請求的首位元組時間是 2.61 秒和 2.26 秒，而旁邊的報告約 0.4 至 0.6 秒。這條路由每次都重新抓取附近成交而沒有快取，雖然報告已抓取過同樣的數據。
- 為何重要: 人們按下「What sold nearby」正開始感興趣，就來到這頁。
- 建議: 按座標快取，或直接使用報告已抓取的數據。

**數據來源的數目因頁而異：13、19 或 14** (首頁、等待頁、/methodology 及比較頁; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 首頁寫「13 official sources」，這是手動輸入的數字，而且把 OpenStreetMap 算在內。等待頁數的是「0 of 19 sources back」。/methodology 列出 14 個機構，卻漏了 Premium 提到的煤炭管理局、NHS England、巴士開放數據服務等。比較頁把我們的檢查與對手的「14 official data sources」並列。
- 為何重要: 細心的初次訪客會核對數字，而在建立信任的頁面上出現三個數目，顯得粗心。
- 建議: 在程式碼中保留一份發佈機構清單，像檢查數目一樣以測試固定。把等待頁的 19 稱為「lookups」。

**「Why trust this?」與報告互相矛盾** (/methodology 及 /areas; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 「What we deliberately don't show」列出棕地，但 Premium 把它列為一項檢查，等待頁也會為它打勾。它說市政稅稅階名單是付費產品，但人手記錄卻為「VOA band lookup」計時 1.3 分鐘。/areas 承諾每個地區都有水浸區域和「Band D council tax from MHCLG」，但水浸區域只覆蓋英格蘭，而 Band D 來自三個政府，北愛爾蘭則沒有。
- 為何重要: 打開「Why trust this?」的是持懷疑態度的人，一個與報告矛盾的頁面會失去他們。
- 建議: 把棕地從不顯示清單中刪去。為稅階給出一個與記錄一致的理由，哪個才是真的由你決定。/areas 按各地實際擁有的數據措辭。

**市場報告把郡稱為城市，諾丁漢的報告用了郡的數字** (/market-report 及諾丁漢地址的報告; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 「18 major UK cities」包括「Nottinghamshire」和「Aberdeenshire」，因為地區配對偏好包含該名稱的最短標籤。NG1 的報告會引用諾丁漢郡的平均價，並如此標示。標題寫「17 September 2026」，數據卻是 7 月的；「Steepest falling: City of Westminster (-20.7%)」沒有任何附註。
- 為何重要: 把郡的平均價標為城市，就是在官方來源名稱旁邊放了一個錯誤數字。
- 建議: 在完全配對之後，優先選擇「City of」標籤，並以諾丁漢和鴨巴甸作測試。在標題寫明數據月份。在大幅變動旁邊加一句關於樣本少的說明。

**學校的措辭像機器輸出，總覽也沒有說明缺漏** (學校頁結論及 Birmingham 入學距離總覽; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 結論寫「N10 3JA is 0.4 miles from Fortismere School. comfortably inside the distance the school admitted from last time (1.883 miles ...)」，句首小寫，而且有三個小數位，旁邊的方格卻寫「1.88 mi」。Birmingham 總覽列出 2023/24 年的 52 間小學，從未說明缺少中學。它的「Sign up to save the schools you care about」會把新帳戶帶回沒有任何儲存按鈕的列表。
- 為何重要: 這是訪問量最高的頁面類型存在的目的，而總覽的缺漏令家有較大孩子的家長空手離開。
- 建議: 句首大寫、保留兩個小數位。加一句說明總覽涵蓋哪個階段和年份。為已登入帳戶在總覽每行加儲存按鈕，並在註冊過程中保留所選學校。

**內容頁與報告之間的死胡同** (學校指南、私立學校、總覽、市政稅、市場報告及置業指南; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: KT3 4HX 的學校指南沒有連到 KT3 4HX 的報告，也沒有儲存或分享。私立學校和總覽的「full property report」、市政稅頁的「Run a free report」和市場報告的「search it directly」全部連回首頁。置業指南指向「the calculator on any property report here」，那是被鎖的，而免費的印花稅計算器卻沒有連結。
- 為何重要: 每次經首頁繞路，都會丟失引發這次點擊的郵區或地區。
- 建議: 連結時帶上郵區或地區，為學校指南加上分享列，並連到免費計算器。

**首頁把展示效果放在信任之前** (首頁，電腦及手機; 工作量 M)

- 我看到的: 在電腦上，示範報告卡放在標題上面，把搜尋按鈕推到 900px 畫面的 y=833，點擊也沒有反應。在手機上它的格子被切掉（「£147,62」）。在三個重點方格和「See pricing for the full list of 44」之間，約有 4.5 個手機畫面是捲動生成的報告和環繞動畫，兩者都在說 44，而準確度記錄到第九個畫面才出現。「Scroll · the page is the scrubber」是影片剪接的術語。
- 為何重要: 焦慮的買家看完標題後的問題是「這可靠嗎？」，不是「有多少項檢查？」。
- 建議: 在電腦上把示範卡放在表單旁邊或下面，並把它變成連結。在小手機上格子改為兩行兩列。兩個「44」展示只保留一個，由你選擇，並把來源段落、準確度記錄和評價移上。

**等待頁列出的是數據集，不是答案** (等待頁，手機; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 各行寫「Nearby sold comparables」、「ONS demographics」、「Noise & air quality models」。在手機上，第九行之後就超出畫面。標題寫「Building the report for BN11EE」，而頁頭的郵區示範在等待期間仍不停打字。
- 為何重要: 十秒足夠讓人切回樓盤網站，而一個看似打錯的郵區，令人懷疑是否搜錯了地方。
- 建議: 每行改寫成買家的問題，來源放後面（「What it last sold for · HM Land Registry」）。郵區用報告的等寬字體並保留空格。在這頁停止打字示範，並把進度數目放在分頁標題。

### 低

**巴士時間寫「first bus 00:19, last 00:14」** (學校頁、地區指南及報告; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: Fortismere：「first bus 00:19, last 00:14.」LS6：「first bus 05:05, last 04:24.」
- 為何重要: 尾班車比頭班車早，看起來像錯誤，懷疑還會蔓延到旁邊的數字。
- 建議: 「頭班」取夜間最長空檔之後的第一班，需要時寫「after midnight」，每小時都有車時寫「runs through the night」。

**細心讀者會發現的小矛盾** (地區指南、市政稅頁及報告彈窗; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: LS6 同一頁的 Band D 既寫「£2,284」又寫「£2,283」，「Run the 44 checks」旁邊又寫「30+ other checks」。Basildon 的輸入框示例是「e.g. S11 8XZ」，那是謝菲爾德的郵區。Band F 在彈窗是 £3,768，在生活開支頁是 £3,769。「Nearby essentials」一次走訪顯示「Data unavailable」，另一次顯示「52 nearby」，其彈窗還露出開發者備註「Not set up on this deployment」。
- 為何重要: 在一個賣精確的網站上，同一件事有兩個數字，會令人兩個都懷疑。
- 建議: 每個數字只用一個來源，全站用檢查數目常數，示例改用本地郵區，顯示「Data unavailable」前重試一次，並刪除開發者備註。

**地區索引在手機上長 42 個畫面，輸入框也沒說可以輸入地區** (/areas，手機; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 議會名稱下有 2,943 個地區連結、2,972 個細小點擊目標和 13 個區域標籤。唯一的輸入框「Check one address instead」其實輸入「LS6」就會跳到地區指南，但沒有任何說明，也無法按城鎮或議會名稱搜尋。第 13 個區域「England」只有「Gedling NG21」一項。
- 為何重要: 地區指南是 Google 帶人來的頁面類型，而這是它們在選單上的正門。
- 建議: 把輸入框標明可輸入地區或城鎮，輸入時即時篩選議會區塊，在手機上收起各區域，並把 NG21 歸入 East Midlands。

**學校頁把同一條問題問兩次** (學校頁（Fortismere），手機; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 相隔一個畫面有兩個「Check this postcode」輸入框，加上頁頭的輸入框。六間最近的學校列了兩次。原始嵌入碼「Put Fortismere School's figure on your own site」夾在巴士時間和私立學校區塊之間。地圖上的地區標籤是 16px 的點擊目標。
- 為何重要: 在一個 11.6 個畫面長的手機頁面上，重複的內容把有用的區塊推下去，也把唯一的行動分散了。
- 建議: 只保留一個檢查框，有答案後再以「Check another postcode」出現；最近學校只列一次；把徽章移到 Common questions 之後；地圖標籤加大到 32px。

**手機上的 Premium 頁長 17 個畫面，大部分是很小的檢查標題** (/premium，手機; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 兩個計劃之後是 44 張檢查卡片，每行一張，標題只有 9.5px，而「Before you pay」在它們全部之後。
- 為何重要: 少數來到 Premium 的人正在考慮付費，而能解決顧慮的答案在十五個畫面以下。
- 建議: 把 44 項按報告的六個分組歸類，按下才展開；標題至少 12px；把「Before you pay」移上。

**「Show every card at once」會被永久記住** (報告，任何裝置; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 按一下之後，這部裝置以後的每份報告都以列表顯示。列表模式下分組方格被隱藏，回訪時跳到有變動分組的功能失效，電腦上卡片標籤只有 11px。
- 為何重要: 跳到有變動的地方，正是網站贏得第二次到訪的方法。
- 建議: 在列表模式下也捲動到並標示有變動的分組，列表標籤至少 12px。

**選單中的「Extension」會被讀成房屋擴建** (頁頭，電腦; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 選單寫「Why trust this, Property, Schools, Running costs, Buying Guide, Extension, Pricing」，而這是一個地產網站，它自己的檢查清單就有一項「EXTENSION」。
- 為何重要: 含糊的標籤會浪費一次點擊。
- 建議: 改名為「Chrome extension」。

**登入頁和 404 頁錯過了時機** (從「我的物業」到 /login，以及 404 頁; 工作量 S)

- 我看到的: 未登入時打開「我的物業」，會去到一個普通的「Log in」頁，完全沒有提到已儲存的房子，而註冊連結會丟失目的地。404 頁在一個手機畫面內顯示兩個郵區輸入框，除一項外所有連結都是學校頁。
- 為何重要: 登入頁是回訪的大門，而回訪正是人們付費的時候。
- 建議: 當訪客原本要去「我的物業」時，標題改為「Your saved homes」；註冊時保留目的地；404 頁隱藏頁頭輸入框，並在連結中加入市政稅和生活開支。

## 網站可以如何更具互動性

網站已經有很多可以玩的東西：分組方格、會打開卡片的結論理由、稅階選擇、成交比較的年份按鈕、地區指南的第二個地區、學校指南的額外地區、學校名稱自動完成、學校頁的郵區檢查和鄰近學校的範圍圈，還有那盞燈。但大部分都差一步，而且我做過的都沒有被保留，所以沒有東西拉我回來。

以下每個構思都使用網站已有的數據，為買家保留一些東西，並按實用程度與工作量排序。免費的用來吸引人；Premium 的那個則提供一個隨房子數目增加而變得更有價值的付費理由。

1. **叫價合理嗎？在成交比較頁** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 類型選項（獨立屋、半獨立屋、排屋、分層單位，土地註冊處的「Other」預設關閉）和業權切換，會與年份按鈕一起重新計算中位數、範圍和標尺；現時年份按鈕只會隱藏資料列，數字從不改變。單看 80 宗半獨立屋成交，£823,500 高於 40%，而不是頁面拿所有成交比較得出的 71%。叫價輸入框用一句話把價錢放到標尺上，例如「£850,000 is above 6 of the 22 semi-detached sales here in the last 2 years」，並顯示換樓人士、首次置業人士和額外物業的印花稅。
   - 所用數據: 頁面上已有的土地註冊處成交記錄（類型、業權、價錢、日期、距離）；網站計算器已有的印花稅稅階
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 每間房子都有叫價。把叫價和已儲存的房子一起保存，是最自然的儲存和重開理由，也令 Premium 的比較功能顯示買家自己的數字。它只為已記錄的成交排序，從不估算價錢。
2. **可以打勾的看樓清單** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 真正的勾選框，每項附一行記下賣家或代理的回答，打勾時儲存在裝置上（「7 of 12 checked, 2 to follow up」）。登入後，勾選和答案存到「我的物業」中的那間房子，並出現在並排比較中。仍然可以列印。
   - 所用數據: 報告自己的發現，經現有的看樓清單功能
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 看樓是買家最投入的時刻，也是第二次到訪的前一步。一份打過勾、有答案的清單，是他們當晚會重開、並拿來和下一間房子比較的東西。
3. **在每個學校頁顯示你看過的房子** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 在郵區檢查下面，列出你在這部裝置上打開過（或登入後儲存）的每個地址，附距離和 Likely、Borderline 或 Unlikely 的判斷，每項連到它的報告。在學校候選名單上，以已公佈的數字列出已儲存房子對候選學校的表格。
   - 所用數據: 議會公佈的入學距離、教育部的學校座標、裝置上最近看過的地方及「我的物業」
   - 回訪或付費的理由: Google 把家長帶到學校頁。一次過得到所有房子的答案，並可開帳戶在不同裝置保留，就是在他們到達的那一頁註冊的理由。
4. **會更新所有數字的稅階選擇** (免費; 工作量 S)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 選 Band F 會更新表格的兩行，而不只是標題；選擇會寫進分享連結，並與已儲存的房子一起保留。一個切換可以把現時能源費換成完成證書建議改善後的費用（KT3 4HX 為 £974 對 £671）。
   - 所用數據: 頁面上已有的議會稅階表及能源證書數字
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 現時同一間房子在相隔一個畫面處有兩個不同的全年總額，分享給伴侶的還是錯的那個。
5. **多年的入學距離** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 議會公佈多年數字的地方（Haringey、Bristol、Bexley 及 Solihull），保留每一年，加年份切換重畫範圍圈，並讓檢查回答「Likely in 3 of the last 4 published years」。附近學校表格顯示年份，而不是「varies」。
   - 所用數據: 匯入程式已讀取、然後只保留一年的議會多年數據表
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 頁面告訴家長距離每年都變，卻只顯示一年。已儲存的學校在議會重新公佈時已會發提示；這樣提示就有東西可以比較。
6. **兩間學校都在範圍內的地方** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 在學校頁加入第二間學校，並可選填預算：列出兩個公佈距離都涵蓋的地區，按成交中位價由平至貴排列，地圖上畫出兩個範圍圈，郵區檢查同時回答兩間學校。
   - 所用數據: 公佈的入學距離、教育部座標，以及地區指南中的各區中位價
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 在還沒有看中房子之前，它回答「我們該在哪裡找？」；儲存這兩間學校會把它們加入候選名單，任何一個議會重新公佈時都會發提示。
7. **我的必要條件，每份報告都會檢查** (付費; 工作量 L)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 從報告已有評級的檢查中選最多六個條件，例如水浸區域、指定學校為 Likely、能源評級、寬頻、每小時巴士班次、Band D 上限或業權。之後每份報告都以「4 of 5 met」開頭，每行打開對應卡片；如果來源不涵蓋該地址，就如實說明。「我的物業」可按符合條件的數目排序。它只在設定條件後才出現，所以不會在第一張卡片前增加任何要求。
   - 所用數據: 報告已從環境署、英國地質調查局、議會入學距離、能源證書登記、Ofcom、運輸部及 MHCLG 計算出的卡片狀態
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 它的價值隨房子數目增加，而兩位訂閱者正是這樣使用網站的。以免費檢查設定的條件保持免費；以被鎖檢查設定的條件，是買家自己選擇的付費牆。
8. **罪案發生在哪裡** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 在報告地圖上加一個顯示當月罪案的圖層，附爆竊、汽車罪案、反社會行為和暴力罪案選項，並註明 Police.uk 的位置點已移到附近街道。
   - 所用數據: 報告已抓取、之後只保留數目的 Police.uk 街道記錄
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 罪案集中在哪裡，是人們會看、也會發給伴侶的東西，而每月的新數據都是再看一次的理由。
9. **一個輸入框接受郵區、地區、城鎮、議會或學校** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 首頁輸入框接受「LS6」、「Kingston」或「Tiffin School」，建議按地區指南、市政稅或學校分組。完整郵區仍然會開報告。現時輸入地區已會跳轉，但首頁和 /areas 都沒有說明。
   - 所用數據: 網站已有的地區清單、市政稅議會及學校搜尋
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 很多買家從一個地方或一間學校開始，而這正是 Google 帶他們去的地方，但現時首頁無法接受。
10. **某間房子附近、適合孩子年齡的私立學校** (免費; 工作量 M)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 在議會私立學校頁加郵區輸入框和年齡選項（「4 歲有學位」、「11 歲」、「16 歲」），並連到學校指南，那裡已提供私立學校與郵區的距離。
   - 所用數據: 教育部登記的年齡範圍、座標及在學人數
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 它把一份只看一次的名單，變成每看一間房子都會用的工具。
11. **只顯示不同之處的地區比較** (免費; 工作量 S)
   - 用戶做甚麼、得到甚麼: 兩個地區屬同一議會時，把相同的議會數字合併成一行，並加入指南已有的地區數字（貧困程度、每小時巴士班次、每位家庭醫生的病人數、租住比例），以及完整比較頁已有的「只顯示不同」切換。
   - 所用數據: 地區指南中來自 MHCLG、運輸部、NHS England、國家統計局及土地註冊處的數字
   - 回訪或付費的理由: 人們實際比較的是相鄰地區，而現時有一半的數字完全相同。

不要加入的東西維持你定下的：沒有彩紙、聲音、自動輪播、聊天機械人或估價轉盤，除了你在 9 月 12 日要求的信任數字外，也沒有數字滾動效果。以上每個構思都只為已記錄的事實排序或核對，沒有一個會估算價錢。

## 本週的速效改動

1. 不要再告訴新帳戶「You've used your free report」，並加上測試。

4. 「全無問題」橫額只列出已讀取的檢查，失敗的檢查說明未能完成。
