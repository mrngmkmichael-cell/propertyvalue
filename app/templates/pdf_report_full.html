How to read this report

The score is a summary of the checks that bear on living here and on resale: schools, crime against the wider area, price direction, flood and other risks, energy. It is not a valuation and not a survey. Every figure below names the body that published it and the period it covers. Where a source holds nothing for this address, that is written down rather than left blank, because a blank reads as "fine" and it is not.

The checks are grouped into ten parts. Part 1 answers every check on one page; the parts after it carry the detail a solicitor, surveyor, lender or the buyer will want to see.

The score

The score out of 100 starts from the checks that most affect living here and resale: the share of nearby schools rated Good or Outstanding, crime against the wider district, the direction of area prices, the property's energy band, and each mapped risk (flood, surface water, radon, subsidence, noise, air, contamination, planning constraints). Strengths add, concerns subtract, and anything a source could not answer leaves the score untouched rather than counting for or against. It is a summary for comparing addresses, not a valuation and not a survey.

What this report is not

It is not a RICS survey, a solicitor's searches, a mortgage valuation or advice. Sources publish on their own cycles, from monthly (prices, crime) to a decade (the Census); each figure carries its period.
