{{ tr("How to read this") }}

Every figure is the median of real recorded sales on the streets around each district's centre, taken from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data, counting homes only. Land Registry's "other" category is commercial property, and including it would put office blocks inside house price ranges.

{{ tr("England and Wales only.") }} {{ tr("Price Paid Data does not cover Scotland or Northern Ireland, which run their own separate registers, so Scottish and Northern Irish districts are absent from this ranking entirely. That is a gap in what we can see, not a statement about those places.") }}

{{ tr("A district needs at least five recorded home sales to appear at all. Below that a median is a number pretending to be a statistic, so those districts are left out rather than ranked on two transactions. That is also why some very central districts are missing: they are mostly commercial and barely any homes change hands there.") }}
