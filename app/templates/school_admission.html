Common questions

What is the catchment area for {{ school.name }}?

{{ school.name }} does not have one in the sense most people mean. Like most English schools, an oversubscribed year offers places outward from the school until they run out. The nearest thing to an answer: the furthest child admitted in {{ school.academic_year }} lived {{ school.miles }} miles away, per {{ school.authority }}.

How close do I need to live to get a place at {{ school.name }}?

Nobody can say in advance, and anyone who does is guessing. {{ school.miles }} miles was enough in {{ school.academic_year }}{% if school.occupancy_pct and school.occupancy_pct >= 98 %}, and at {{ school.occupancy_pct }}% full the school has little slack{% endif %}. The distance moves every year with the number of applications, so treat last year's figure as evidence, not a promise.

Does buying a house near {{ school.name }} guarantee a place?

No. {% if school.admissions_policy and "Selective" in school.admissions_policy %}This is a selective school, so admission turns on the entrance assessment before distance is considered at all.{% else %}Admission criteria usually put looked-after children, siblings{% if school.religious_character and school.religious_character not in ("None", "Does not apply") %} and faith criteria{% endif %} ahead of distance, so places can be filled before distance is reached.{% endif %} Councils also check that the address is genuinely where the child lives.
