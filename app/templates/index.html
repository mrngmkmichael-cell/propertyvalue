Why trust this

Every figure names its source

Sold prices from HM Land Registry. Flood zones from the Environment Agency. School ratings from Ofsted. Subsidence from the British Geological Survey. Nothing is scraped from a listings site, and nothing is guessed.

Where a source does not cover an address, the report says so, rather than leaving a gap that looks like good news. When a reader tells us a figure is wrong, we check it and publish what we find on the accuracy log, including the times we were wrong.
