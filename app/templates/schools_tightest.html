Where the figures come from, and what they cannot tell you

Each distance is the one the council itself published after offer day, for the last child admitted under the distance criterion: usually a PDF or spreadsheet on the council's admissions pages, sometimes a Freedom of Information release. The source for each council is named on its page. We never model a distance where none was published, and a council with no figures is simply not here.

Three cautions. Only oversubscribed schools get a figure: a school that took everyone who applied has no distance, so its absence means it had room, not that it is unpopular. Councils measure differently: most use a straight line from home to the school gate, a few use the shortest walking route, and a few round to three decimal places while others round to one. And the distance moves every year with the birth rate, the housing built nearby and the number of siblings, so last year's figure is a guide to next year's, not a guarantee. How admissions actually work explains the rest.

Journalists and researchers: the figures on this page and on every council page may be quoted with a link to the page they came from. For the underlying spreadsheet or a figure for a school not shown, email support@ukpropertyinsight.co.uk.
