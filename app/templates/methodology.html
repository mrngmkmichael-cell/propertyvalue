What we deliberately don't show

No free official source exists for these. We say so rather than estimate.

Council Tax band The VOA's banding list is a paid product.
Live "for sale" listings Licensed to Rightmove and Zoopla. We show Land Registry sold prices instead.
School catchment areas (most of the UK) Most English schools have no fixed catchment. We show the council's boundary where one exists, its last-distance-offered figures where not, and a labelled estimate as the last resort.
Service charge / ground rent amounts Private lease data, disclosed only to a solicitor. We show freehold or leasehold from Land Registry.
Brownfield land status Around 300 separate council registers, no national feed.
Full planning application history No national API across England's planning authorities.
Actual sunlight hours / overshadowing Needs LIDAR shadow modelling. We show which way the garden faces instead.
Corporate/overseas property ownership Land Registry's dataset sits behind a licence click-through.
