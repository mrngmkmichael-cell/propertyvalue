{# What the bill above is not. Every council tax figure on this site is the full charge for a band, which is what MHCLG and the devolved governments publish. It is not what a large share of households actually pay: the single-person discount alone takes a quarter off, and on 11 Sep 2026 not one of the 350 council pages, the running-costs page or the report said so. Only statutory rules that apply across a whole nation are stated here, with the government's own page named. Nothing is modelled and no figure is estimated: what a particular household pays depends on who lives there, which no dataset holds. Empty-property and second-home premiums are deliberately absent, because each council sets its own and there is no national figure to quote. Takes `nation` ("England", "Wales" or "Scotland"). #}

Most households pay it; a good many do not. One adult living alone pays 25% less. A home where every resident is disregarded, a full-time student, under 18, an apprentice, a live-in carer or severely mentally impaired, pays 50% less or nothing at all, depending on who they are. A home adapted for a disabled resident is charged at the band below.
