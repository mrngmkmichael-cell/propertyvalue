How it works

1. Add to Chrome One click, no sign-up required to install.
2. Browse a listing Open any Rightmove, Zoopla or OnTheMarket property page as normal.
3. See the data instantly A summary bar appears automatically — click it for the full tabbed report, no extra searching.

Free to install, more with an account

Market History, Comparables and Schools show a free preview on every listing. Log in with a UKPropertyInsight account — Premium unlocks the full lists and every other card the main site's own property reports have.

Nothing extra running in the background

The extension only activates on Rightmove, Zoopla and OnTheMarket listing pages, and only reads the postcode from the page — nothing else. No ads, no analytics, no tracking of your browsing elsewhere. Full detail in the privacy policy.
