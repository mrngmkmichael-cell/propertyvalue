What each does better than this site

  • Propbar shows planning applications and decisions around a property, offers an AI researcher over the record, and its free extension adds days on market and floor areas to listings. It also lists three more checks than this site does.
  • Crystal Roof charges nothing at all, and its statistics for a postcode give noise sources and air pollution their own categories, with traffic, aircraft, emergency services and nightlife listed separately.
  • Locrating has published school data since 2010 and shows where a school's existing pupils live, its feeder and destination schools and its priority areas, which this site does not hold. Its members rate it on the site itself.
