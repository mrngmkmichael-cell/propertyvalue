What the bill depends on

The band is fixed to the home by the Valuation Office Agency from its value in 1991 (2003 in Wales) and does not change when the house changes hands. The charge for the band is set each spring by the billing authority, and includes the precepts of the county, the police and fire authorities, and any parish. Discounts (single occupancy, students, some disabilities) come off the figure shown. That is not the whole cost of living somewhere: running costs by postcode covers the energy estimate on the EPC, tenure, and the estate charge no council collects.
