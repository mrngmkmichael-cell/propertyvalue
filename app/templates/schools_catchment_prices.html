How this is worked out, and what it cannot tell you

Distances are the ones each council published after offer day, for the last child admitted under the distance criterion; their sources are named on each council's page. Prices are the median of the last twelve months of Land Registry sales in each postcode district, the same figures as the area guides, and only districts with enough sales to give a fair median are used. A district counts as within reach when its centre falls inside the school's distance. When a distance is shorter than the gap to any district centre, the district the school stands in is used instead.

A district median is not the price of a house near the gate: a district can be a mile across, and the streets closest to a popular school usually sell above its median. The distance moves every year. And only oversubscribed schools carry a figure, so a school missing from these tables had room for everyone who applied. How admissions actually work covers the rest; every school's own page has a postcode checker and its priced districts.
