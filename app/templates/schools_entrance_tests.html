What this page is, and what it is not

These are the arrangements for entry in September {{ summary.entry_year }}, which means the round that ran through 2026. Registration for most areas closed in June or July and the tests were sat in September, so for a family moving now the useful part is the shape of it: how early the area registers, what the papers contain, and how long after the test the result arrives. The next round's dates appear on the same pages in the spring.

Nothing here is inferred from another area. Where a council does not name its test provider, this page says it does not, instead of repeating what a tutoring company claims. Where no results date is published, it says that too. Every area lists the pages it was read from, so anything on this page can be checked against the body that published it.

Sitting a test is not an application. In every area the school still has to be named on the local authority form, which closes on {{ summary.national.secondary_applications_close | day_label(true) }} for secondary places, with offers on {{ summary.national.secondary_offer_day | day_label(true) }} (gov.uk). A qualifying score is not an offer either: several areas say in as many words that more children reach the standard than there are places.
