School catchment areas

There's no free UK-wide catchment-area dataset — most English school admissions (especially secondaries, mostly academies now) don't have a fixed catchment at all; places go by priority criteria with distance only as a tie-breaker, which shifts every year. This first checks for a real, published catchment-boundary shape from the handful of local authorities that openly publish one; where none exists, it falls back to nearby schools' admission-distance figures instead — a real published number where the council has one, or a clearly-labelled modelled estimate where it doesn't. A real boundary shape is each council's current boundary, not a historical year-by-year archive, since none of these sources publish past years' shapes either.
