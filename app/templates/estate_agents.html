Who manages your estate?

Every managed estate has a company behind it, registered at Companies House, and where that company is registered tells you a lot: {{ "{:,}".format(data.attributed) }} of the {{ "{:,}".format(data.total) }} active residents' and estate management companies in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland are registered to the office of a managing agent. This is the register read for that, monthly, under the Open Government Licence. It says who sits behind an estate; it does not say what residents pay, because no official source does.

The offices most residents' companies are registered to

A company registered to an agent's office is almost always one that agent administers, which is why this is the nearest thing to a league table of estate management that exists. The register records the address, not the contract, so the wording throughout is "registered to", never "managed by".
