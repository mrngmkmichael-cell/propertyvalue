# Named families

Extracted from the seven references collected on 25 Aug 2026. The folders
are named after their Dribbble shots; these are the *styles* inside them,
which is what can go into a prompt. Point at a family by name.

---

## The headline finding

Six of the seven references lead with **an image of a building or a
person**, and five layer **floating white cards over a coloured or
photographic ground**. The live site has neither: it is text, tokens and
tables all the way down.

So the gap between what Michael likes and what the site is today is not
typography or palette (those are already deliberate and right). It is
**imagery and depth**. That is the direction worth testing.

---

## `property-photographic`
*AI Real Estate Property Management Dashboard, Roofing Contractors*

A real building carries the hero; UI cards float over it with soft
shadows. Warm daylight, generous crop, the product visible *on* the
photo rather than beside it.

Why it fits: the product is about actual homes, and the site currently
shows none. Risk: stock photography would undo the credibility the data
earns. Would need real UK housing imagery, honestly sourced.

## `stat-row`
*AI Real Estate ($24.8M / 127 / 89%), Roofing (15+ / 2,400+ / 25-Yr)*

Three or four figures in a row, set large, each with a tiny uppercase
label beneath in a second colour. Does the work of a paragraph.

Why it fits: "23 checks · 13 official sources · 1.7M postcodes" is
already on the landing page as a thin text line. As a real stat row it
would carry weight.

## `numbered-index`
*Roofing (01/02/03 with a vertical hairline), Trading ([STEP 1])*

Sections marked with a mono number and a thin rule rather than a
heading. Quiet, editorial, orders a page without shouting.

Why it fits: already proven in the PDF report, where numbered sections
replaced the icon squares. Free to bring to the web.

## `mono-halftone`
*Sendlytic*

Warm greige ground, photography reduced to black-and-white halftone, one
solid black pill as the only CTA. Colour appears nowhere else.

Why it fits: adds imagery *without* introducing a second palette, which
is the main risk of going photographic. Closest to the current site.

## `search-first`
*Home Services & Plumbing*

The hero is the search bar. Below it, a mosaic of tiles with photos and
labels acting as the results you could have.

Why it fits: this is literally the product. The area-chip strip on the
landing page is a first step toward the mosaic half.

## `dark-terminal`
*Trading Platform*

Near-black ground, one vivid accent (green), soft glow shapes, stacked
translucent cards, mono bracket labels.

Why it fits: not as the main site, but this is the reference to build
**dark mode** from when that comes.

## `cinematic-3d`
*Prima Booking, Delight*

A fully rendered 3D scene as the entire hero, cinematic lighting, UI
floating over it.

Honest note: off-brief. Expensive to produce, dates quickly, and reads
as marketing rather than record-keeping. Collected because it is
striking, not because it suits this product.
